# How reports, requests and moderation are handled

This is how the maintainers handle what arrives in the repository. It describes how things are done; it promises no response times and no dates.
<!-- owner to confirm: the whole page is a recommended default -->

## Asking for a feature

Open a **Discussion** in the *Ideas* category, not an issue. Say what you are trying to do, what you do today, and
why that falls short. Other people can add their experience and vote.

Maintainers read the Ideas category. An idea they decide to build is planned by them and then appears in the
roadmap and the release notes; the discussion is marked as answered and says so. An idea they decide not to build is
closed with the reason. Ideas are not turned into issues: issues are for bugs, debt and chores that a contributor
can take. A feature you want to build yourself starts as a discussion too; see
[Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Reporting a bug

Use the bug report form. Agenthix is in early access and maintained by a small team with limited time. When a maintainer looks at a report, they do one of these:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `bug` | Confirmed, or reproduced from the steps given. |
| `question` | More information is needed. The report may be closed if the information does not arrive. |
| `duplicate` | Another report covers it; the report is closed with a link. |
| `invalid` | Not a defect, or not in this repository. It is closed with the reason. |
| `wontfix` | A real behaviour that will not change; closed with the reason. |
| `good first issue`, `help wanted` | A small change a contributor can take. |

There is no promised response time and no promised fix date. Early users who triage, reproduce and help others are
what makes this project work, and are warmly welcomed. A security problem is reported privately (see
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md)).

## Pull requests

A pull request needs a linked issue or an agreed discussion, the checks passing, and a review. Maintainers say so on
the pull request when it will not be merged and why.

## Moderation

Everyone follows the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or delete an issue,
discussion, comment or pull request that breaks it, or that is spam, advertising or off topic, and say why when they
can do so without harming anyone. A report of conduct goes to the address in the code of conduct, never to a public
thread; the reporter's privacy is kept. The code of conduct's four steps apply in order: a private correction, a
warning, a temporary ban and a permanent ban. A serious or repeated violation, or harassment, may go straight to a
ban.
