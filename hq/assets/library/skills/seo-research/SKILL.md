# SEO Research

SEO supports content — it doesn't replace good writing.

## Keyword research process
1. Start with the topic and brainstorm seed keywords
2. Check search volume and difficulty for each seed
3. Look for long-tail variants with lower competition
4. Check search intent: are results informational, navigational, or transactional?
5. Select 1 primary keyword and 2–4 secondary keywords per piece

## On-page recommendations
- Primary keyword in title, first paragraph, and one H2
- Meta description: 150–160 characters, include primary keyword
- Internal links: 2–4 links to related content
- Image alt text: descriptive, include keyword where natural

## Deliverables per piece
- Keyword recommendation with volume and difficulty
- Suggested title (SEO-optimised)
- Meta description
- Any structural recommendations (e.g. add FAQ section)
