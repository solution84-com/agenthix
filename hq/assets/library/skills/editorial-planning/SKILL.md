# Editorial Planning

Every piece of content starts with a brief.

## Content brief template
- **Topic**: what is this piece about?
- **Goal**: what should the reader do or know after reading?
- **Audience**: who is this for?
- **Format**: article, guide, script, post, etc.
- **Length**: approximate word count
- **Deadline**: when is the first draft due?
- **Keywords**: 2–4 primary keywords (for SEO pieces)

## Editorial calendar
- Plan content 2 weeks ahead minimum
- Each slot in the calendar links to a brief
- Track status: Briefed → Drafting → In Review → Published

## Topic research
- Check search volume before committing to a topic
- Review competitor coverage for gaps
- Confirm the topic aligns with current product/marketing goals
