# Stakeholder Communications

Keep stakeholders informed without overwhelming them.

## Weekly status update
- What shipped this week
- What is in progress
- Any blockers or risks
- What is planned for next week

## Scope changes
- Never change scope silently
- State what is changing, why, and the impact on timeline
- Get explicit sign-off before proceeding

## Roadmap
- Keep it up to date after every sprint
- Mark completed items, move incomplete items, add new ones
- Three horizons: Now (this sprint), Next (next 1–2 sprints), Later (backlog)
