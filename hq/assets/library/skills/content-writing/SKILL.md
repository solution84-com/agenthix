# Content Writing

Write from the brief. Every word should earn its place.

## Before writing
1. Read the brief fully
2. Research the topic — understand it better than the reader needs to
3. Outline the structure before writing prose

## Writing
- Lead with value — tell the reader why this matters in the first paragraph
- Use short sentences and paragraphs
- Active voice over passive
- Concrete examples over abstract claims

## After the draft
- Read it aloud — awkward sentences stand out
- Cut anything that doesn't serve the goal
- Check against the brief: does this deliver what was asked for?
