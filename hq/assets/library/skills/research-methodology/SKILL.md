# Research Methodology

Good research starts with a clear question and ends with a defensible answer.

## Framing a research question
- Specific: what exactly are we trying to find out?
- Answerable: can we actually gather the data needed?
- Relevant: does the answer change what we do?

## Scope definition
- What is in scope: sources, time range, population
- What is out of scope: explicit exclusions to avoid scope creep
- Assumptions: things we are taking as given without proving

## Reviewing findings
- Does the conclusion follow from the data?
- Are alternative explanations considered and ruled out?
- Are the limitations stated honestly?
- Can the methodology be reproduced by someone else?
