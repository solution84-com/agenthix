# Research Reporting

A research report must be understandable to someone who didn't do the research.

## Report structure
1. **Executive summary** — the key finding and its implication (1 paragraph)
2. **Background** — why this was researched
3. **Methodology** — what data, what methods, what assumptions
4. **Findings** — what the data shows (use charts and tables)
5. **Conclusions** — what the findings mean
6. **Limitations** — what this research cannot tell us
7. **Appendix** — raw data, full query list, detailed methodology

## Writing principles
- Lead with the finding, not the method
- Use plain language — spell out acronyms, avoid jargon
- Every chart needs a caption that states the finding
- Separate facts from interpretations clearly
