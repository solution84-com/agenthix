# Content Review

Editing is making good writing great, not rewriting from scratch.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Does the opening hook the reader?
- [ ] Is the structure logical — does each section lead to the next?
- [ ] Is the tone consistent with the brand voice?
- [ ] Are all factual claims accurate and sourced?
- [ ] Are there any sentences that could be cut without losing meaning?
- [ ] Is the call-to-action clear?

## Feedback approach
- Be specific: quote the problematic text, explain why it needs to change
- Separate blocking issues from suggestions
- Approve only when all blocking issues are resolved
- Turnaround: review within one working day
