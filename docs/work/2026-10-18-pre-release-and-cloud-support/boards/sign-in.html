Sign in with Agenthix Cloud — the sign-in page

Spec part D, first bullet; part C2. The button is always on HQ's sign-in page. It works when HQ is connected to Agenthix Cloud; otherwise it is greyed out with its reason beneath. The page is public, so it carries no link to Cloud and says nothing about Cloud beyond the button.

Today

  1. The sign-in page shows the organisation's cover, picture, name and handle across the top, then Sign in to Acme Robotics's HQ: username, password, Remember my username, Forgot your password?, the Sign in button, and New here? Ask an admin….
  2. There is one way in: HQ's own username and password. Nothing mentions Agenthix Cloud.
Before
Username and password only.
After
Under Sign in, an or divider and a full-width Sign in with Agenthix Cloud button. Connected: it starts the OpenID Connect sign-in (authorization code with PKCE) at Cloud and comes back signed in. Not connected: greyed out, with one line beneath saying why and what to do instead. Never the only way in: the password form stays above it.
Why
Spec D: always present, so a person learns it exists; greyed with its reason, so nobody presses a button that cannot work. Placed under the password form, because most people in a self-hosted HQ sign in with a password and should not have to look past Cloud to find it.

Sign in with Agenthix Cloud — the invitation page

Spec part D, first bullet. The same button on the page every invitation opens (onboarding's Join, and setup's first step for the first admin).

Today

  1. Join: progress (Step 1 of 3 · Join), Join Acme Robotics, who invited the person and to what, then name, username and password, the Join button, and Already have an account here? Sign in. Beside it, a few people already in the organisation.
  2. Setup's first step (the first admin's link): our mark, Step 1 of 4 · You, name, username, password, Continue.
Before
Name, username and password.
After
Under the Join button, or and Sign in with Agenthix Cloud, greyed with its reason when HQ is not connected. Connected: the person signs in on Cloud and comes back to the same form with their name filled in from Cloud, the password field gone, and a line saying they sign in with their Agenthix account. They choose a username for this HQ and press Join; the new account is linked to their Cloud account at once.
Why
Spec D says the invitation page carries it too. The person is making their own account here, so linking it at once is the same act as C2's linking after signing in as oneself. HQ still needs a username of its own: it is how people are named and mentioned inside HQ.

Built from · onboarding/pages/steps/JoinStep.tsx and setup/pages/steps/AccountStep.tsx pass the same CloudSignInButton under AccountFields; AccountFields gains a withoutPassword mode and a name default. Data: the invitation read (readInvite, InviteView) gains cloudSignIn: boolean; acceptInvite takes an optional proof of the Cloud sign-in (a short-lived token kept by hq between the OIDC return and Join, never sent to the browser as a Cloud token) in place of the password, and links the account it makes.

Open, decided conservatively for the owner: Whether the invitation's Cloud path makes an account with no password (drawn), or still asks for one. Drawn without, because C2 already expects people with no HQ password (they are sent a link to set one if HQ disconnects). And whether setup's first step should show the greyed button at all on a fresh self-hosted HQ — drawn, because the spec says always; it could be left out until HQ is connected.
