The organisation switcher — in HQ's menu

Spec part D, third bullet. For every member, at the top of HQ's menu, listing only this organisation. Not connected, it says other organisations need Agenthix Cloud and why; connected and linked, Switch organisation… opens the person's switcher on Cloud. It always holds Start your own organisation ↗.

Today

  1. The top of the menu is the product's mark and name, then Notifications and Runs. Under it, Home, Chat, Your space, Projects, Agents, Tasks; at the foot, the person (a menu with Organisation, Settings, Sign out) and Collapse.
  2. Nothing in the menu names the organisation the person is in; there is no way to reach another.
Before
Mark, panel buttons, then the places.
After
One row under the mark: the organisation's picture and name, with ⇅. It opens a small menu listing only this organisation, ticked, then what Cloud would give and the way to it, then Start your own organisation ↗. Collapsed, the row is the organisation's picture alone.
Why
Spec D: every member sees what Cloud gives (switching between organisations, an organisation of their own) where they would look for it. Listing only this organisation keeps the promise that HQ never learns the person's other organisations (C2): Cloud draws the real list, on Cloud.

The same switcher in the Fleet Console's menu head, under on hq-box · 127.0.0.1. The person is signed in with their HQ account, so it shows what HQ shows them.

Before
Mark, bell, badge.
After

Why
Spec D places it in both. A fleet admin is a member too, and the Fleet Console is where connecting happens.
