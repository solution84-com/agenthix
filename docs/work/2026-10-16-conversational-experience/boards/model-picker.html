Why

  • The model is where you are talking, beside the agent's name, so you can see it and change it without leaving the conversation.
  • Search finds a model by any part of its name. Grouping by who makes it (Anthropic, DeepSeek, OpenAI) matches how people think of models; the key that pays is on the group's right (“via OpenRouter”).
  • Only models one of your keys reaches are listed, within the organisation's allowed list and ceiling — so nothing in it fails when chosen.
  • Each group shows its best-known few and “Show N more”; Recent keeps the two or three you use on top.
  • $ to $$$ is a rough price hint; the switch line says the next reply reads everything again, which is the one cost of switching.
  • The same picker replaces the long list in Settings → Models.
