Kernel's answer

How the agent's turn, its work and its results read in a conversation: the design settled in the walkthrough of the conversational experience, and the baseline the build follows. Kernel is the built-in agent; WAL is a sample project, Walkthrough.

1 · Thinking — the turnThe pill on Kernel's name line, with a pulsing dot while it runs. Its steps: what Kernel looked up and what it started. Kernel reads them back next turn, so it never redoes work.
2 · Kernel's words — alwaysEvery turn says something. Starting work: one short line in the same step (no extra call); hq writes a fixed one if the model wrote none.
3 · The work bar — detached workOne bar, the last thing in the chat: all live work and every request for your okay, collapsible, scrolls. Kernel says nothing when a request arrives.
4 · Cards — what completedA piece that finishes, fails or is stopped leaves the bar: a card, then Kernel's reply, its own block. A task for a time completes in the turn.
Your turn = the dot stops · Copy · Read aloud · ⋯ appear · Stop turns back to the voice buttonEach turn is its own blockThe app bar loses its live count and sheetAccent bar = written by hq
