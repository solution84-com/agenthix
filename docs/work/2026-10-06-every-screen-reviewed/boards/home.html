The proposal — how Home balances what a person wants with what they should see
1. Talk firstThe composer is the screen's centre and has focus when Home opens — a person can type the moment they land. It starts a conversation in Chat with the agent they used last.
2. Signals, not listsWhat they should know is one line of chips: what needs them, what's running, what's coming up. Each count opens the place to act on it — notifications, Runs, Automation. A list on Home they can't act on only competes with the composer.
3. Then where they were"Jump back in" is the one list: their last conversations from every project and Chat. Most visits are a return to something already under way.

Everything else in the catalogue has a better home: numbers, cost and top agents on an Insights screen for whoever watches them; running work in Runs, coming up in Automation; pins already in the navigation.

The app shell — today's pieces and where they go
TodayIn this design
Top bar breadcrumbsThe bar at the top of each screen: breadcrumb on the left, the layout control on the right where the screen has tools. No global top bar.
Notifications (bell, top bar → right panel)Bell beside the logo, with a count, opening a panel over the page. What needs action also leads Home.
Chat (icon, top bar → right panel)Chat in the navigation — the personal space. Conversations open as screens, never in a side panel.
Activity (icon with count → right panel) — it lists runsRenamed Runs. In the navigation with a live count, opening a Runs panel beside it (glance and stop); a Runs screen for the full list; the Runs tool in each project and in Chat; the organisation-wide Stop all moves to the future Sites view. The dashboard's run-event feed folds in.
Voice call controls (chat panel)Voice in the conversation's composer.
The global right panelGone. The right panel only ever holds a project's or the personal space's facts and tools.
Dashboard: stats, cost, financial, top agentsThe future Sites view (organisation-wide), designed later.
"My tasks" in the navigationGone — the Tasks tool in Chat.
Where we are

Carried from the agreed boards

  • Navigation: Home, Chat, Projects, Agents, Tasks, Runs; Pinned below; the person and Settings at the foot.
  • Light by default, dark available; the agreed type scale.
  • Activity is renamed Runs (it lists runs); a Runs screen holds the full list, reached from the panel.
  • Home and the Runs panel are personal: what you started or what acts on your behalf. Organisation-wide views — runs by site, the organisation's Stop all, today's dashboard numbers — go to a future Sites view, designed later.
  • Navigation: Home · Chat · Projects · Agents · Tasks, then Pins; Organisation and Settings at the foot. Boards and Workspaces leave the navigation (inside project and Chat tools, and pins); Products, Programs and Library move into Organisation, with a product/programme filter on Projects.
  • Chat-first Home: composer in the centre with focus, one line of signal chips, Jump back in. Nothing else.
  • Tasks waiting for you raise a notification (small, new) — needed now that "needs you" lives in notifications.
  • Notifications and Runs stay separate: what needs you vs what is happening.
  • Notifications and Runs get the same treatment: two buttons at the top of the navigation, beside the logo, each with its count, each opening its panel beside the navigation. Collapsed: the first two icons under the logo. Mobile: both in the header, top right.
  • What needs you lives in notifications — the bell, with its count; Home only says how many and links there.
