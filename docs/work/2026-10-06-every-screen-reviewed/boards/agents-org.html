Agents — managing (Organisation › Agents)

Where the organisation's admins manage its agents: create them, shape who they are and how they work, assign them to projects, and see what they cost everyone. Agents are the organisation's "employees"; this is their side of the organisation's settings. Using an agent — talking to it, seeing what it does for you — is under Agents in the navigation, on the board Agents — using an agent.
