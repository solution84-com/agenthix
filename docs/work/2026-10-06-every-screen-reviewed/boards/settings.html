Settings

Where settings live, and whose they are. Three owners, three places, kept apart: you (Settings), the organisation as a workplace (Organisation › Settings, its admins), and the infrastructure everything runs on (a separate admin portal with its own sign-in, its own admins). An organisation admin never manages infrastructure — that is too risky — and the infrastructure can later move to a hosting service as a whole.

Agreed

  • Settings is only the signed-in person's own; anything for admins goes to Organisation (owner, 2026-10-09)
  • The infrastructure — HQ itself, every Execution Site, storage — is its own concern with its own admins, signing in to a separate portal, never the organisation's admins: too risky otherwise, and it can move to a hosting service whole (owner, 2026-10-09)
  • Until host management exists, the infrastructure settings stay in the app as Organisation › Infrastructure, its own entry on the Organisation page beside Organisation › Settings, never inside it (owner, 2026-10-09)
  • Model and voice providers stay the organisation admins' for now, as they work today. Who holds model keys — the person a run is for, the project, the organisation or the host — is decided with host management (owner, 2026-10-09)
  • Each setting has the one owner the table shows — you, the organisation, or the infrastructure (Organisation › Infrastructure for now). The infrastructure's own sign-in and admins are a future build with host management, not this pass (owner, 2026-10-09)
  • It is called Infrastructure (owner, 2026-10-09)
  • Organisation › Infrastructure is for organisation admins only until host management gives it its own admins; members do not see the entry, and its address tells them only admins can see it (owner, 2026-10-09)
