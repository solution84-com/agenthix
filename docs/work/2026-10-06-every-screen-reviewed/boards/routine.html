A routine

A routine repeats a piece of work: on its schedule it creates the next round, an ordinary task, on behalf of the person who set it up (adr-0092). A person opens one to see whether it is keeping up — the next round, and how the last ones went — and, less often, to change what it creates or when. It opens inside the Automation tool (‹ Routines), like a board inside Boards.
