A person's own Profile is part of the Settings pass.

Roles — Viewer moves to the project (2026-10-09)

Organisation roles

Admin·Member
  • Admin runs the organisation: people, agents, projects, its settings
  • Member everyone else
  • Viewer is no longer an organisation role

Project roles

Admin·Member·Viewer
  • Admin runs the project: its people, agents, settings
  • Member works in it: talks to its agents, starts and changes work, approves
  • Viewer reads it and changes nothing

Why

  • All work is inside a project, so whether someone may work is the project's to say
  • Read-only in one project, fully involved in another
  • No project Admin who cannot chat because of an organisation role

Notes

Agreed

  • Agents and people are not tools: a People row and an Agents row after the place's facts, spaced apart with no heading, before the tools; Settings alone at the foot; the same on desktop and phone (owner, 2026-10-08)
  • An open project you have not joined shows a preview — what it is, its people and agents, how busy it is — and Join; not its conversations or tools (owner, 2026-10-08). Words about a person are gender-neutral (owner, 2026-10-08)
  • When someone leaves: Remove deactivates, Reactivate brings the same account back, Erase deletes their own data and keeps shared work as "Former member"; no automatic erase after a set time (owner, 2026-10-08). Today's delete loses project task runs, which blocks release; an audit log is for later
  • Organisation admins may add, promote, demote and remove people in every project (owner, 2026-10-08)
  • Open projects in this pass: anyone may Join in one step; until then they read but do not act; the member list is the truth. A decision record (owner, 2026-10-08)
  • People get a display name and a picture, as agents do (owner, 2026-10-08)
  • Operator is shown as Member: Admin · Member · Viewer (owner, 2026-10-08)
  • An invitation's description ("Who it's for") shows in the list; projects they join on accepting may be chosen, optional (owner, 2026-10-08)
  • People is the next design pass (owner, 2026-10-08)
  • Viewer is a project role, not an organisation one: organisation roles are Admin · Member; project roles are Admin · Member · Viewer. All work is inside a project, so whether someone may work is the project's (owner, 2026-10-09). A decision record replacing adr-0062's roles
  • A project Viewer only reads: everything a member reads, and nothing else — no conversations with its agents, no work started, stopped or changed, no approvals, no comments, nothing of its people, agents or settings (owner, 2026-10-09)
  • Join makes you a Member, as before (adr-0099); a project's admins may make anyone a Viewer. A project that wants newcomers read-only is private, with people added as Viewers (owner, 2026-10-09)
