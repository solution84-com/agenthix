Notifications and Runs

The two panels beside the navigation. Notifications answers "what needs me, or concerns me?"; Runs answers "what are agents doing for me?". Today each item is a card of four to six lines, a waiting run is filed under "Finished recently", and the same waiting task appears in both panels. This draft gives every item two lines and one action, and puts what waits for a person at the top of each.
