2 · Option A — recommended

What agents can do first; providers as one line each

  1. What agents can do moves to the top: the answer first, the accounts behind it after.
  2. Your providers become a table of one line per provider: name, kinds, runtime, state. A working one is quiet; one that isn't working shows its line in the error colour with its reason.
  3. Pressing a provider's line opens it in place, with today's full card (key, what uses it, Check now, History, Replace key, Rename, Remove, Revoke). One open at a time; an alert's link opens that provider's line already open.
  4. Which models may run stays folded at the bottom.

Six providers take about 300px instead of 1,500px, and the summary is visible without scrolling. Setup gets the same one-line rows (with Remove), and keeps its list after the providers, since setup is about adding them.

2a · Proposed

Pressing a provider opens it in place

The line unfolds into today's full card, right where it is: state and since when, runtime, key, what uses it, the vendor's message when it isn't working, and Check now · History · ⋯ (Replace key, Rename, Remove, Revoke).

One provider open at a time; pressing another closes the first. Pressing the open line closes it.

An alert or notification about a provider opens the page with that provider's line already open.

Why in place: the details belong to the page, not to a moment that needs your attention, so nothing covers the page; the list and What agents can do stay where they were; it works the same on a phone; and the link from an alert can land on it.

2b · The alternative

…or a pop-up with the provider's details

The same card in a dialog over the page.

Against it: it covers the list and the summary while you look; a dialog is for something that needs an answer now (hq's message rules keep pop-ups for that); an alert's link would have to open a dialog on arrival; and Replace key and Remove already open their own dialogs, so you'd get a dialog on a dialog.

3 · Option B

Providers on the left, What agents can do pinned on the right

Today's cards stay as they are in a wider left column; the capability list sits in a right column that stays in view while the providers scroll.

Against it: the page still scrolls as long; below about 1,100px wide the two columns stack and today's problem comes back (phones, a narrow window, the docked conversation open beside it). The cards still carry most of their detail for providers that are fine.
