7 · What it stops, what it does not, what waits

The honest edges of the design, how it is proved, and what is left for later on purpose.

What it stops

  • A tool call reaching another place's knowledge: there is no place to name (board 5).
  • A change to a page with no author, no previous version, or that silently overwrites another writer (board 4).
  • An agent answering a question about Agenthix from guesswork, when it was told to look it up and the answer is in the guide (board 3).

What it does not stop

GapWhy it staysWhat limits it
A misled agent carrying one place's knowledge into another of the conversation's places, unaskedWithin the places the person added to the conversation: the blast radius they chose (board 6, C3)The tools reach only the conversation's places; every brief shows in the conversation
An agent writing something wrong, or something it was tricked into, into a pageAgents writing knowledge when asked is the pointEvery change shows who and why; history and undo; agents write only when a person asks (instruction)
A person carrying knowledge between their own projectsIt is theirs to shareNothing: their call, as if they had pasted it
Platform knowledge describing a feature a run's tools don't allowThe guide describes the product, not a runEvery guide read starts with "what you may do here is what your tools allow"

TestKind

V4The gate: write and edit, T1–T5, a changed page refused, an edit's passage not found exactly once refused, properties kept as written, versions, pruning, permanent deletion, restore; the itemiser lists hand-placed files with their problemsUnit (site), integration (hq and site)
V5The place rule: every knowledge tool reaches only the run's place or the conversation's places, the organisation and the guide; start_work refuses a place not in the conversation; only the person adds a place; a place they lose access to drops out; org: changes only for adminsUnit and cross-app, in the security suite
