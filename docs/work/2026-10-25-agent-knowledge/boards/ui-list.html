  1. The tree shows the place's sections, as the team laid them out, with their page counts; a page at the top level shows in the tree too.
  2. A row: title, description, tags, and the team's properties (here a decision's status and date), when it changed and who.
  3. Filters: Tag, and one per property the section's pages use (here status). Nothing about decisions is built in; a section of client sheets would get its own.
  4. A row with problems shows them in place of the description and is gathered under Needs fixing. It is titled from its file name until it has a title.
  5. All pages lists every page, newest first; the tree narrows it to a section.
  6. New page opens the editor (U3) in the section shown, with the properties the section's other pages use already offered. For people who may change the place's files Q4. ⋯ has New section.

Why

  • A tree of sections: people find their way the way they do in Files or a guide.
  • Properties shown and filterable, so a team keeping decisions here gets a decision log (status, date) without the platform knowing what a decision is.
  • Problems shown, never hidden: a page with something wrong still appears, with what to fix.
