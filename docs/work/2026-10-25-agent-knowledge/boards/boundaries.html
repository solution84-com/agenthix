6 · Who reaches what

Level 2. Each level of knowledge is reached only by those allowed to. A conversation reaches only its own place and the places its person added: the person sets the blast radius, deliberately (C3).

The blast radius: the places the person added

After · proposed

A conversation starts with its own place (a project, or the person's own space), the organisation's knowledge and the platform guide. Any other place is added by the person, in the conversation's header, like Claude Code's additional directories (C3). So whatever a conversation moves between places, it moves between places the person chose to put together: they accept the blast radius, by a deliberate act they can see and undo.

Mechanism
P1hq stores each conversation's places: its own, and each added one with who added it and when.
P2start_work's admission (admitToBoundary) accepts only a place in the conversation; today it accepts any place the person can reach.
P3The lead's knowledge tools search and read every place in the conversation, each hit marked with its place (board 5). A worker or a task run has one place, as today.
P4Adding checks the person can access the place, then and on every turn: a place they have lost access to drops out of the conversation.
P5suggest_place is the lead's only way to ask for one: a card the person answers.

No check on briefs (D14): within a conversation's places, carrying knowledge across is the person's choice.

Why

  • The tools hold the line that matters: an agent can never reach a place the person can't.
  • Workers find their own place's knowledge, so a brief no longer needs to carry it; most of the old reason to copy knowledge into briefs is gone.
  • Simple and honest: no partial check that looks like protection and misses paraphrase.
