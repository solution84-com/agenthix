C3 · Places in a conversation

A conversation sees its own place, the organisation and the platform guide. Any other place is added by the person, deliberately, like Claude Code's additional directories. The person sets the blast radius, not us. This changes how conversations work.

Before and after

Before · today
  • The lead may start work in any place the person can reach (start_work, admitToBoundary), from any conversation.
  • Nothing on screen says which places a conversation is reaching.
After · proposed
  • A conversation reaches its own place, plus the organisation's knowledge and the platform guide.
  • Other places only when the person adds them, shown in the conversation's header, removable at any time.
  • The lead may suggest one; only the person's click adds it.

  4. The work step for a search names the places it covered, so the person always sees how far it reached.

Why

  • The person accepts the blast radius by a deliberate act they can see and undo; nothing reaches further than they chose.
  • Like additional directories in Claude Code: a session sees its own folder until you add another.
  • The lead may ask, never add, so a misled agent can't widen its own reach.
