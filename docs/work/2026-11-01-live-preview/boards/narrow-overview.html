Narrow screens: what folds first

The side panel folds first and the conversation last, so the conversation stays in view as long as there is room.

1Wide · 1280 px and up — Everything shows: the left panel, the middle, the conversation.
2Laptop · about 1100–1280 px — The left panel folds to its strip; the middle and the conversation stay.
3Tablet · below about 900 px — The conversation becomes a drawer from the right, opened by “● Kernel is working” in the top bar; the preview’s bar stays in view (F14).
4Phone · about 390 px — One thing at a time, as HQ does on a phone today: Conversation first, then Live preview, Files and Git from a switch at the bottom; the branch select is the header’s title.
