The idea, against the tabs

Today (built, in this unit): a place's sections are tabs across the top of the canvas, folding into ⋯. The alternative: the left panel becomes the place while you're in it, listing every section down the side with its count, and the breadcrumb's parts open lists. The canvas gets the whole height, and you always see where you are. The dock on the right is the same either way.

5 · Leaving the place: ‹ shows the main navigation over the panel

Owner's idea (2026-10-18): keep a back button at the top of the drilled-in panel. Tapping ‹ doesn't go anywhere: it slides the main navigation over the panel, with the place you're in still marked. Choosing an item goes there. Dismissing it (✕, Esc or a click outside) goes nowhere — you're exactly where you were, same section, same scroll. Nothing navigates until you choose.

Open questions

Q4 is in section 5. Answer by number.

Answered (owner): Q1 A, with the back button. The panel's top is the place's header — ‹, avatar, name, pin; the name opens Overview; the canvas has no header; the switcher is the breadcrumb's Agenthix ▾.

Q1 · The place's header in the panel: avatar, name and pin, and the canvas loses its header

With tabs, the header (avatar, switcher, pin) sits above the tabs. Drilled in, the panel's top already names the place, so the canvas header would repeat it.

A The panel's top is the header: avatar, name, pin; clicking the name opens Overview. The canvas has no header (the switcher is the breadcrumb's Agenthix ▾).Recommended
B Keep today's header above the canvas as well.
Answered (owner): Q2 A. Group labels in the section list, as drawn; nothing nested.

Q2 · Small group headings in the section list (Work, Agents at work, Files, Project)

You didn't want sections grouped under sub-tabs. These are only labels in a vertical list — every section stays one click away — and they help scan 14 rows.

A Labels, as drawn; no nesting, nothing hidden.Recommended
B One flat list, no labels.
Answered (owner): Q3 A. Overview keeps the facts, Members and the sections as cards with counts.

Q3 · Overview, now that the panel lists every section with its count

Today's Overview is the facts plus the sections with counts. The panel now shows the sections and counts all the time.

A Overview keeps the facts and Members, and shows the sections as cards with their counts (as drawn), since it's where a place opens.Recommended
B Overview keeps only the facts and Members; the sections are the panel's job.
Answered (owner): Q5 A. Lucide icons, one per section — drawn under Section icons.

Q5 · An icon for every section

The collapsed panel needs a distinct icon per section (14 for a project). Today only some have one.

A Pick from the icon set already used (lucide), one per section, shown on the board for your look before building.Recommended
B The collapsed panel shows the place's avatar only; sections come from the breadcrumb's list.
Answered (owner): Q6 A. On a phone the header shows the section and its title opens the section list; the drawer has the same ‹ (the main navigation inside the drawer), and tapping outside the drawer dismisses it. Artefacts' icon is a diamond.

Q6 · Phone: the header's title opens the section list

A Yes, and the drawer shows the drilled-in panel too.Recommended
B The drawer only.

How it's built and backed out

Answered (owner): Q7 — a temporary switch for development. "better to keep one or the other. But during development, let's have that switch so I can play around with it first. And then decide whether to use the new navigation or the tab." The one not chosen and the switch are removed before the unit closes.

Q7 · Comparing it with the tabs

A A temporary switch, remembered in your browser, between tabs and the drilled-in panel, so you live with both on the same screens; the one you don't choose and the switch are removed before the unit closes.Recommended
B Replace the tabs outright, as one merge; backing out means reverting it.
Either way, nothing inside a section changes, the addresses stay (?tool=), and the dock is untouched.
