1 · The draft opens (＋ or Talk about Agenthix)

The dock swaps to a draft; what was open is remembered. The whole area is the list of places you can use, with search at the top. The place you're on is already selected and the message box has focus, so for the usual case you just type and send: no taps.

2 · Searching for a place

Typing in search filters the list; the groups fold into matches. The selection doesn't move until you choose, so search never changes where the conversation goes by itself.

4 · Cancel

Cancel (or Esc) puts back exactly what was open before, scroll and unsent text included. If nothing was open, the dock closes. Anything typed in the draft and not sent is discarded, with no question.
