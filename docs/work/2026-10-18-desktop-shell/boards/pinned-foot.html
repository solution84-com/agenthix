5 · Recommendation

Recommended

Pinned as one row at the foot, and B: one menu behind your name

  • Pinned never moves, so tapping it keeps you oriented: the problem you saw.
  • The foot is two rows instead of five, so the place's sections get the room back.
  • Organisation, Settings and Sign out are used rarely; one click into a menu is the usual pattern (Slack, Linear, Notion, GitHub all put them behind you).
  • Collapse stays a one-click button on your row.
  • No server change: open/closed and the last pin are remembered in the browser.

A: Pinned at the foot, the other rows kept

  • Fixes the moving pins just as well.
  • Costs about 170 px of sections at all times; on a laptop that's 5 sections pushed into scrolling.
Question 1: Pinned as one row at the foot (as drawn in 2), with B: Organisation, Settings and Sign out in a menu behind your name, Collapse kept as a button?
