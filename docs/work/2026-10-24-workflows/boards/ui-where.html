After · proposed
Projects › Website rebuild
Website rebuild
  1. Workflows, in Agents at work, after Automation: what agents and people can run with the project's secrets. Its count is the number of workflows, or "N need you" when versions wait for approval (U2).
  2. Secrets and variables, beside Members and Settings: two tabs, secrets encrypted and never shown, variables plain and visible (U6).

Why there

  • Workflows are agents' work, like Runs and Automation, so they sit with them. Automation already shows On or Off as its count; a workflow's own switch reads the same way.
  • Secrets and variables are the project's set-up, like Members and Settings: set once by an administrator, rarely visited.
  • "N need you" uses the panel's existing needs tone, as Tasks does, so a waiting approval is seen without opening the conversation it came from.
  • A person's own space (/space) has the same two sections, for workflows and secrets in their own storage.
