Why

  • A conversation is narrow; reading a long script in it is how things get approved without being read. So a small change that moves no secret can be approved from the card, and anything else must be opened.
  • The reviewer can be steered by text in the workflow (board 5, R3), so its level never decides what the person is shown: a steered "low risk" can't put a one-tap Approve beside a change that hands a secret to new code.
  • The reviewer's points come before the code, so the person knows where to look.
