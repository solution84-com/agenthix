Settled design

Overview: what changes

future state

The conversation is where people spend most of their time, and today it hides what the agent is doing, says failures in a developer's words, opens long answers at their end, and loses an agent's request for permission altogether. The answer keeps the panel as area 2 settled it and changes what is inside it.

The problems, and the answer to each

  1. An agent asking permission in a conversation shows nothing (capture 34 to 39): no card, no bell, nowhere to say yes → the card appears in the thread where it was asked, and a Waiting for you strip sits above the message box until it is answered (Approvals). This is a fault, not a layout question: it is raised as a fix on its own.
  2. "Thinking" is the only word for the whole run → a live line says what is happening now (Searching Knowledge…, Writing…), and one list of steps under it is the only record of the turn's work; what it made shows once, as a small card at the end (A run in progress, Steps and what they made).
  3. Failures are worded for developers and lead nowhere → each says what happened, who can fix it (worded for the person reading), and offers Try again; a half-written answer is marked as cut (When something goes wrong).
  4. A long answer opens at its end → a new answer opens at its start; ↓ New answer shows when one arrives below; the start of the conversation is marked (Reading and scrolling).
  5. The header puts the title last → the title is the biggest thing; one line under it says who leads, where it lives and what else it reads; the menu says what each thing does (The header and its menu).
  6. "+" only changes the model → the model is its own chip in the box, as when starting a conversation, with a list that searches and puts recommended and recent first; Enter and Shift+Enter are said while typing (The message box).
Asks you (they add something the product does not do today, so they are yours to decide; each screen marks where):
  1. Rename a conversation from its menu (today the title is the answer's first words, for good).
  2. Try again on an answer that failed or was stopped (today: type the message again).

Every improvement, numbered

What is off today, and the change for each

  1. The order does not match the times: 16:30 sits above 18:30 and 19:30 under "newest first" (the list is ordered by the last message, but the time shown is from another one) → newest first by the time shown, and the two always agree. A fault: fixed whatever else is decided.
  2. Unread is a small badge clipped onto the avatar's corner, and otherwise only bold → the count sits at the right of the row as a pill, the title is bold and the time is in the accent colour; read rows are plain.
  3. Three lines a row, the place a third grey line → two lines: title and time; and the last message. The place is its tile alone, on the agent's avatar (the owner, 6 Nov: no place names, they take too much room); its name is the tile's tooltip. One more conversation fits in every three.
  4. The open conversation looks like a hovered row → it keeps a pale fill and gets a bar of the accent colour at its left.
  5. Day headings: Today, Yesterday, then the date, so the times on a row can stay short.
  6. "Select to mark read or archive several at once" is a blue sentence across the list, read as a link → a Select icon at the end of the filter row, with that sentence as its tooltip.
  7. Filters of four shapes (two chips with counts, two drop-downs, an Archived chip), and Needs you 0 shown with nothing in it → All and Unread as chips, Needs you only when something needs you, and project, agent and archived in one Filter ▾; the button shows how many are set.
  8. The subtitle "Every conversation you can open, newest first" says what a list is → removed.
Why. A chat list is read in a second: who, what, when, and whether it needs me. Today four kinds of marks compete (badge, bold, a blue sentence, a pale fill) and the order contradicts the times. Two lines, one unread mark, one filter button and day headings are what people know from every messaging app. The same rows are used in the chat panel's list, so both change together.
