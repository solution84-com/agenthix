Plan maintenance — one panel, everything visible, nothing starts over

Agreed with the owner on 2026-10-16, after trying the first build live, and built that day. It replaces the earlier Scheduled board and each action's Now, or schedule for later?, marked as superseded where they stand. Spec §14, §15; the redesign's pages are on The redesign.

What this is

  • Maintenance is the work done to HQ or a site: an upgrade, a change of capacity, a move, a suspend — now, or at a time members are told of.
  • Plan maintenance opens as a panel beside the page you are on, like the notices panel, so the list — or the site — stays in view. Which and What are drop-downs, always there and always changeable; the chosen kind's fields appear below; When is a clear choice of two, saying what members will see; one button whose words follow When.
  • It opens from + Plan maintenance on the Maintenance page, and from the one Plan maintenance… button on HQ's page and on each site's page. Erase stays its own button on HQ's page.

Before

  • First, each change was its own action on the machine's page, three levels deep, and the Scheduled tab only listed what was planned.
  • Then a Make a change page of its own: 1 Which → 2 What → 3 the form, ending in a small Now / At a time…. Pressing Change… on a step started over, so the person lost their place; from a site's page, Change capacity… and Upgrade… each opened it with a different thing preselected, and once there you could not switch between them. The button's words changed with each form.

Schedule for Sat 02:00
  • What each thing offers: HQ — Upgrade, Change capacity, Move, Suspend; a site — Upgrade, Change capacity, Move; HQ's own site — Change capacity only, since it upgrades and moves with HQ. Choosing a thing that does not offer the chosen kind clears What and says why ("hq-box upgrades with HQ: choose HQ for that.").
  • A thing that cannot take work now — not answering, in a move, or HQ suspended — says why in its hint.
  • The button reads Do it now, or Schedule for the day and time; it is in the danger style for Suspend. The machine's answer — working, done, scheduled, refused — shows in the panel.
  • A move started in the panel is followed on its own page, and a move's notice opens its outcome.
Why

Nothing starts over. Every choice stays on screen and can be changed in place; changing What keeps Which, and changing Which keeps What when the new one allows it. There are no steps, so there is no place to lose. What was typed is kept while the panel is open; closing it forgets it.

A drop-down shows the current choice and the others at once — you always see where you are and what else you could pick.

The page you came from stays visible beside the panel, so you can check what is already planned, or the site's figures, while deciding. Closing it leaves you exactly where you were.

One door from a site: a single Plan maintenance… button that knows which site, instead of a button per change that each pre-chooses a different thing.

When says what members will see, and the button says what will happen: Do it now or Schedule for Sat 02:00 — the same words for every kind of maintenance.
