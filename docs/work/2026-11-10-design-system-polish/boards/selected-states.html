2 · The selected row in a menu or list

The sidebar, the folder tree, picker options. The fill is grey; the text tells you it's chosen.

Proposed: fill and ink
Overview Tasks Boards Sprints

Rows that aren't chosen are the softer secondary grey. The chosen row's text and icon go to full ink in medium weight on the grey fill, so it's the strongest thing in the list: the opposite of inactive. This is what ChatGPT's sidebar does.

6 · Cards and tiles

A card you can open lifts on hover: its edge firms up and a soft shadow appears (in dark, it also goes one shade lighter). It doesn't move or change colour. A card that only holds information doesn't react at all, so the hover itself tells people what's clickable.
