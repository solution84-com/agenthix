Why it feels less polished today

  1. Dark is tinted blue-violet and nearly black: #0a0a0f, #12121a, #1a1a26, steps of 1.06 to 1.08:1. The references use neutral #181818 and #212121.
  2. No field token: Input, Select and Textarea paint surface-2, and Main.css forces surface-2 !important on every text input. That is the grey field on a white page. 56 files have grey fields and 73 have white ones.
  3. Too many outlines: 12 bordered controls and 18 hairlines on the task form. The dark border #2e2e45 is bluish.
  4. The accent does too much: selection, links, file names, open panels, the composer focus. It also misses AA: 4.17:1 as text on dark, 4.47:1 for white on the button.
  5. Selection is indigo, not a grey step.
  6. The text greys are violet (#b8b8cc, #8c8ca4), and some text is pure white.
  7. Type is tight: 13/18 is the most common size, message text renders at 14.25 px, and headings are 600.
  8. Seven radii appear on one screen, and controls are a slightly hard 8 px.
  9. The failure card is alarmed: a red fill and a red border.
  10. Menus sit at page tone, with boxed selects inside.

Tokens: today and proposed

Every text level reads at 4.5:1 or more on every surface it meets. Secondary stays 1.5:1 or more from muted.
