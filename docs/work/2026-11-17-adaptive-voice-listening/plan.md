# Implementation Plan · Adaptive voice listening

## Goal

Build the design in [`spec.md`](spec.md): adaptive voice as three services over a foundation — Reasoning (the
heavy model: the answer, turn joining, the recap), Responsive (the light model: who speaks when, and something said
within a second) and Backchannel (listening sounds while the person talks) — with an optional Tone layer, each layer
picking the tone of its own speech. Behind a flag, with the voice playground able to try every part of it.

**In scope:** the measurements the spec lists; the light model's module, its actions, parser, caps and rules; hq's
listening call, the fallback and the stored hold phrases; the recap; the heavy model's voice instructions; the
browser's listening runtime, the floor and pacing; the backchannel and its listening read; tone per layer and one Tone
switch; the listening style as a Reasoning action; Adaptive voice off as plain voice; the voice playground with every
number as a setting; a report and walkthrough.

**Out of scope:** "Still with me?" and a bid to come in (designed later, after the six tools have run); moving a reply to
background work and messages sent while a reply runs (their own later work); several speakers; candidate models beyond
DeepSeek Flash (the owner's call for this run: the baseline only); removing the old path (it goes when the flag
graduates, after the owner's live trial).

## Approach

**Measure before building, on one model.** The owner set DeepSeek Flash via OpenRouter as the only model for this run,
for both the light and the heavy model, with a hard stop at $10 of spend (expected $3–5), tracked in
`execution/reports/spend.md`. Phase 1 drafts the light model's instructions and tools as the same pure module the
product will use, and runs the spec's test sets against it. Where a measurement shows part of the design does not hold,
the spec's safe default is taken and recorded here as a deviation and in the report: plain JSON instead of tools; a
short pause after words instead of a sentence end; a separate small call for the recap.

**One module, two hosts.** The light model's instructions, tool definitions, parser, caps and rules are a pure module
in hq with no database and no Wasp, the way the lead's voice logic is today (`conversations/lead/voiceLogic.ts`). hq's
listening call and the voice playground both run it, so what is tried is what ships.

**Behind a flag with a stage.** With the flag off, voice is exactly today's — hold, opener and timed listening family
included. They are removed when the flag graduates, after the owner's live trial, not in this unit. Versioned surfaces
(the client protocol's voice events) move their version, and `turnHeld` and `trailedOff` are marked deprecated.

**Carried out by lanes, without the owner** (owner's instruction, 17 Nov 2026). Each phase is one or more lanes: one
purpose, one agent with the model chosen for it (Opus for prompt, protocol and race-prone work; Sonnet for bounded
build work), one worktree, landing on `feature/adaptive-voice-listening` as one squashed commit, rebased and
fast-forwarded. Phases 2, 3 and 4 share the voice protocol and run in sequence; phase 5 follows phase 4. Lanes run
scoped tests for what they changed; the lead runs the integration suites for each lane and the full regression once at
the close. At most five agents run the app at once. Every phase commits on the branch as it lands; nothing is pushed or
merged — the owner reviews the report and decides. The run ends with a report and a playground walkthrough with
captioned screenshots (kept out of git), then the Mac is shut down.
