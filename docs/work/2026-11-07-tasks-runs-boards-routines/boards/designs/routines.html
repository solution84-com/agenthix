#What it isWhyFrom
9Repeats: presets in wordsEvery N minutes · Every N hours · Daily at · Weekdays at (one or more times) · Weekly on (day chips) · Monthly on day N · Monthly on the first or last weekday, each with its fields. Choosing a row, or typing in one, picks it. Every N minutes refuses fewer than 5, said under the field: a refusal known in advance sits on the control, not in a banner.D22
10Advanced: a cron expressionA text field for anything the presets don't cover (for example 0 9,15 * * 1-5); picking a preset fills it in, and a cron a preset can say selects that preset. A step under 5 minutes is refused on the field, with why.D22
11TimezoneThe routine's timezone, from your own by default.D22

On a phone

The same screens at 390 px: controls 44 px. The top bar holds ☰ (or Back on a routine), the title, chat and ⋯; the screen's own actions are behind ⋯, the main one first (New routine on the list; Run now, then Rename, on a routine) and “Website relaunch menu ›” last. A routine's On switch stays in its content, beside who it runs for. New routine is a focused page: ✕ cancels, and Create routine is its one filled button. The counts and Up next scroll sideways, a card keeps its avatar column with the name and “on behalf of” beside it and ⋯ at the end, and ordering moves into ⋯ (Move up, Move down). The routine's Details follow its instructions; the builder is full width, and in it the whole row picks its choice, so the target is the row, not the radio.
