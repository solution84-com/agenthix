The rail, and the places across projects

High-fidelity screens for D20: the rail's eleven places in three groups, with a divider between them. Boards, Workspaces and Routines are places in the rail, each a list across projects; the Projects page lists projects only. This replaces the earlier version, where Boards, Workspaces and Routines were tabs on the Projects page. Sam Okafor is signed in to the organisation Fernhill.

The rail: eleven places in three groups

Home · Chat · Your space | Projects · Boards · Your tasks · Workspaces · Routines · Workrooms | Agents · Library, each a 36 px icon named by its tooltip. Here every tooltip is drawn at once so each name can be read; in the product one shows, on hover or focus. The foot holds notifications, Runs and you, as today.

On a phone

Below the wide layout the rail is the menu (☰) drawer: 300 px from the left over a scrim, the organisation with ✕, the same eleven places as rows in the same three groups with dividers, and a foot with Notifications, Runs and the person. The top bar holds ☰ (with a dot when something needs the person), the title, chat and ⋯; the screen's actions are behind ⋯. The Projects page is its cards, one to a row. This drawer is the one every phone screen opens.
