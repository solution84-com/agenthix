Workrooms: your agents at work across projects

In the rail after Your tasks. The same counts and scorecards as a Workroom, for your runs only (started by you or on your behalf), grouped by project (each in its project’s agent order), and your own space last. Start is greyed here (“Start from the project’s Workroom”); Stop every run… stops every run of yours. No filled button: nothing here creates or starts. On a phone Stop every run… is in ⋯ (the second phone shows it open).

A run’s own view: its steps, what it runs on, what it cost

One view of a run wherever it opens (the panel, the Runs pages, a task’s Runs tab). Drawn for a task’s run at work, then a routine’s run that asks you something, a failed run and a stopped one. No box to type into a run: the conversation steers. On a phone a run is a sub-page (Back, not ☰) and its actions are in ⋯: Stop first, then Open the task (the second phone).
