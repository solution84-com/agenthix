Designs: tasks, runs, boards and routines

The approved high-fidelity designs of every screen the unit changes, drawn from the approved wireframes (the wireframe board). Each file has its own Light and Dark switch and a switch that shows the numbered parts of its key. They share one shell, described in kit/README.md; where a design and a wireframe differ, the design is current.
