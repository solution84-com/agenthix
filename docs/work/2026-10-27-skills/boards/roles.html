Every role against every action, as decided in D17, D24–D28 and D30. This table is what the role-by-action tests check, one test per cell. Screens: U2–U5, P1, R1, R2.

Today

  • Organisation roles: ADMIN and MEMBER (hq/src/server/identity/rolePolicy.ts), checked by requireRole (requireRole.ts). Every skill action today needs ADMIN (hq/src/agents/actions.ts).
  • Project roles: ADMIN, MEMBER, VIEWER (ProjectRole, hq/schema.prisma).
  • Agents hold no authority of their own: an agent's call is checked as its person's browser request would be (rolePolicy.ts), and a run with no person fails every floor.

History, reach and departure

  • History records every action above: who (a person, or an agent with its person and run), when, and the note or reason. Installed assets keep it in hq; a place's skills in hq's per-place record (FV2).
  • Reach (D28): naming a Library maintainer shows once that their published versions run for every agent in every project, including ones they can't access. Accepted risk, stated in the decision record.
  • Departure: removing a member drops their Library maintainer role and their personal skills' kept content (M6); the history still names them.

Before, after, why

Before

Administrators do everything; nobody else can touch a skill (T9).

After

Administrators govern; Library maintainers look after the Library; projects publish their own; everyone can propose.
