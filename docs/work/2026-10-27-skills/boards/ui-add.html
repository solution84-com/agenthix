Before, after, why

Sources

Before

Import takes ZIPs; Install to Org copies from the built-in library. Nothing is checked.

After

Add: from Agenthix, upload, or a link, all through one check.

Why: skills come from anywhere (D7), and nobody is tied to a service. One check means one place where a tampered package is stopped.

Trust

Before

Anything imported is trusted silently.

After

Four states. The person is always told the two things that matter: that it's unsigned, or who signed it.

Why: Agenthix vouches that a signer is who they say they are, never that an asset is safe (D8). Naming the publisher on first trust gives administrators a word they recognise (D9).

Open on this board

None. UQ7 (the "I've read it" tick before installing an unsigned asset) was answered by D26; Install stays disabled until it is ticked. UQ8 (install, then add to agents, as separate steps) and UQ9 (trusted publishers managed in settings) were applied as defaults.
