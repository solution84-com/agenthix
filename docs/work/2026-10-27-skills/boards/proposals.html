States

A Library asset or a place's skill

  • Publish takes the exact revision the publisher saw, refused if the draft changed since (H5); every draft change records its author or proposing run.
  • Withdrawn is the publisher's mark on a version: hq warns before installing it, never refuses (D30).

A skill found in files

  • Switches are keyed by place, path and fingerprint (H3); staging re-reads the folder and stages it only if its fingerprint equals the one switched on.
  • The organisation ships off by default (H4, C1); a place may only be stricter (C1b). Under on by default, new and changed run at once.
  • A person's skills follow them only at a version they published (H4); an unpublished change stays in Your space.

Before, after, why

Before
  • An agent writes a folder and it runs next turn, for everyone (T6).
  • The skills engineer's codify-skill promises a proposal nothing receives.
After
  • Every agent change is a draft, a proposal, or a file change that waits for a publish.
  • One tool, routed by who asked and where it aims.
Why one gate: whatever path an agent takes, a person who may publish at that level is the one who makes it run, and the history says who.
