  2. Versions: each published or installed version, with who, when and a note (D11, V2, V6). Roll back to this makes that version the one in use, after a confirmation, and records who did it. Content of every version is kept while it's in the history.

Before, after, why

A page instead of a dialog

Before

A dialog with the content and Install to Org, plus Edit and Delete for skills made here (U1 ④⑤). No versions, no record of who changed what, no way to see where it's used.

After

A page with its own address: content, who has it, versions, credit, who can edit, and its signer and source.

Why: looking after an asset needs history and ownership (T9, D11, D17, D19). A page can be linked, from a run's step (C2) or from an agent's list (A1).

Agents with this skill, from the asset

Before

To the whole organisation from the Library, or to one agent from that agent's page, built-in skills only (U1 ⑥⑦).

After

Both from here: every agent, or chosen agents. The agent's page offers the same from the other side (A1).

Why: agents have only what someone added (D14). One control, reachable from both ends.

Open on this board

None. UQ4 ("last used by"), UQ5 (a template's agents made from it, read-only) and UQ6 (removing a skill a template brought is allowed, with the warning) were applied as defaults.
