Each setting: before, after, why

Trusted publishers (P1)

Before

Nothing is checked (2).

After

A list of signing identities and keys, each with the name an administrator gave it. Most are added at install time, from S2's Trust this publisher; a key can also be added here for offline signing (W2). Agenthix can't be removed.

Mechanism: each entry stores what a signature proves: a keyless certificate's repository and workflow, or a key's fingerprint. It also stores the administrator's name for it, who added it and when. The install check (spec part 3) matches a verified signature against this list to decide between S1 and S2. Removing one doesn't uninstall anything (RQ8).

Unsigned assets (P2) and who installs from outside (P3)

Before

Any administrator imports anything.

After

Unsigned: allowed after the S3 warning, or never. Installing from an upload or a URL: administrators, or Library maintainers too.

Mechanism: two values the install check reads before it starts. Never refuses S3 at the check. P3 is enforced on the server, beside the role-by-action matrix (D17). Assets made in hq are never "from outside".

Library maintainers: one organisation role (D28)

Before

Only administrators touch skills.

After

Library maintainers here, for every asset. No maintainers per asset. Each project's skills are published by its administrators. Maintainers per team or department may come later.

Mechanism: one organisation role, checked on the server by the same helper as the role-by-action matrix. Naming someone shows the reach once, and that trust is recorded as an accepted risk. Proposals are routed to the reviewers of the level they aim at (R1). When a member leaves, the role goes; the history still names them.

Skills found in files (C1)

Before

Always on, unseen (3).

After

On by default, off by default, or never. A place's own default (C1b) may only be stricter. Switching one skill is on the place's Skills page (board P1).

Mechanism: hq resolves the effective default per place (the organisation's, or the place's if stricter). It combines that with each skill's switch, remembered by fingerprint, into the list of file skills a dispatch names. Never stops hq asking the site to scan at all.

Personal skills outside Your space (D15)

Before

A person's skills apply only in Your space.

After

On by default: they follow the person into their own conversations. Off keeps them in Your space.

Mechanism: when hq builds a run for a person's own conversation outside Your space, it adds their switched-on skills only if this is on.

Built-in skills (D6)

Before

None exist (4).

After

Two, on for every agent, with a switch each.

Mechanism: the switch is one organisation-wide flag per built-in identity. hq leaves a switched-off built-in out of every run. It's never editable here: to change one, an administrator installs their own skill (D18).
