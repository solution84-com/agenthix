Before, after, why

Stale copies

Before

Each install is a copy. Only an agent's own copies show Update (U1 ⑧). The organisation's copies never learn their library version moved on.

After

One installed version per identity, pinned. Update available, with what changed, wherever it's installed.

Why: an out-of-date copy once told agents to push directly (T2). Pinning keeps behaviour stable; offering updates with the difference keeps it current on purpose (D11).

Editing

Before

Built-in skills can't be edited. Skills made here are overwritten in place, with no history.

After

Another publisher's asset: your own copy, with credit kept. Your own: versions, with roll back.

Why: edited content can't carry the original signer's name (D10, D18), and whoever shared it keeps their credit (D19).
