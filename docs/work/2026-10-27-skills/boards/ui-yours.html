New skill and Edit open the editor (U3b) on a draft; a folder written outside the editor is in files, not published or changed in files, not published until you publish it or put the last version back (FV4).   2. Follows you: for the person's own conversation in another place, hq reads the switched-on skills of Your space's two folders through the person's site and delivers them by fingerprint (D15). Before
  • A list of names inside one agent's page.
  • Only in Your space.
  • Live as soon as written.
After
  • A Skills page of your own, with switches.
  • Your way of working comes with you into projects, under the project's standards.
  • You choose what an agent wrote for you before it's used.
Why: people bring their own way of working to every project (D15). Copying a skill into ten projects, or asking for it to become the organisation's, is the friction this removes. It's safe because a skill adds no power: in your own conversation it can only ask for what you could type.
