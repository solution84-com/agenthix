The repository a team already shares on GitHub

The person: Your space today; following the person from now on (D15)

A person's own space is a place, so their skills are L4 and L5 in their storage. They apply only when an agent works in Your space. They do not follow the person into a project, the way ~/.claude/skills follows you into every repository on your machine.

Decided (D15): they follow the person into their own conversations everywhere. hq delivers the turned-on ones by fingerprint. They never apply to runs other people start, or to scheduled ones. The organisation's installed version wins a clash.

How Claude does it: built in or installed

Claude's rule: a skill that is present is on. Installing it is what makes it present. The control afterwards is switching it off, never switching it on.

For Agenthix: two kinds shipped by Agenthix.
  • L1a Built in is like Claude Code's bundled skills: on for every agent, an administrator can switch one off, and nobody edits it.
  • L1b Offered is a shelf, like the API's pre-built skills: nothing runs until it's installed.
After that, Claude's rule applies to everything installed (L2–L6): present means on, and the control is switching off, per organisation, agent or place. A natural first built-in is a skill creator, like Claude's, which teaches an agent how to write and propose a skill well.

What the map shows

  • Three levels are hq's (L1–L3), and three are files (L4–L6). hq's levels are definitions that administrators manage. File levels belong to whoever can write the place, and that includes agents. That is the trust question in Q3.
  • L6 is the one teams already have. A repository with .claude/skills or .agents/skills works in Claude Code and GitHub Copilot today, but not in Agenthix.
  • L4 and L5 are Agenthix's own folders, in the place's files/, not in a repository.
  • No "local" level. As in Claude Code, a per-person, per-project layer would switch skills on or off. It would not hold skills.
