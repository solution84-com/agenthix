Robots · Faces · Initials for an agent, Faces · Initials for a person, Symbols · Initials for the organisation. The styles of the chosen category sit in one row that scrolls sideways. Proposal B · Recommended

Choose a look first, then fine-tune it

The Avatar tab opens on a gallery: every style this subject may use, drawn large on its own name, under its category's heading. That is the real decision — "what would Writer look like as …" — and it is made by looking, not by reading names. Choosing a look opens its fine-tuning (robot grid, parts, background, mirror) with ← All looks to go back. Adding a style later adds a tile, never crowds a row. Trade-off: one more click to reach the parts; Surprise me and Save work from either view.
