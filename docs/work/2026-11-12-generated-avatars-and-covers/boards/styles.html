Which drawn styles does the picker offer?

Every public-domain (CC0) style that draws a face or a figure, each shown for six sample names. Robots stay first for agents; these sit beside them, and they are what people choose from. Click every style you want offered. The spec has the first three today.

Size is what the browser loads when someone opens that style in the picker, compressed; the server image carries them all (about 5 MB for every DiceBear style).

What does an organisation's symbol look like?

A new category for the organisation, and possibly for teams: a symbol, not a face or a robot. Each style is shown six ways, then as the organisation's avatar beside its name. Click the ones the organisation's picker should offer; the first one you pick would be the default at setup.
