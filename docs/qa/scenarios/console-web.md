# Scenarios — `console-web/`

> Module contract: what `console-web/` (the Fleet Console's screens) promises the fleet admins who use them. Flat capability list. The server behind them, `console/`, has its own file, [`console.md`](console.md).
> **Scenario ids are unique across every file in this directory**, not per file, because a test cites a scenario by its id alone; the next free id for all of them is `next-sc` in [`hq.md`](hq.md). Ids are append-only: assigned once, never reused or renumbered.
> Written on 2026-10-16 from the finished code at the close of the host management unit. The stories these fulfil are in [`../stories/console.md`](../stories/console.md).

The screens talk only to the Fleet Console's own API. Every action that changes a machine is a button in a page's header or in the Plan maintenance panel, and every refusal is shown in the host service's or HQ's own words.

---

## Signing in

- **SC-892** — The sign-in page names the account it needs and where the Fleet Console runs; while HQ does not answer nobody can sign in and it says what to run on the machine, and turns to that state if HQ goes away between the page and the attempt; with nobody holding the role it says how a machine admin gives it; a wrong password is said as HQ said it and the password is cleared; an account without the role is told it isn't a fleet admin and who can give it; a right login opens the shell on the page asked for; a session that ends while open returns to sign-in and says why. · fulfils AC-299a
- **SC-893** — The CSRF token is kept in the tab's session storage only — never local storage, never a cookie — and sent on every request, reads included; the cookie alone opens nothing; a reload keeps the session while the tab keeps its token; a 401 forgets the token and says why, but a wrong password at sign-in is not a session ending. A new tab signs in from an open one: it takes the token a signed-in tab answers with and checks it with the server before opening the shell, shows sign-in when no signed-in tab is open or the server no longer takes the token, and ignores answers to another tab's ask; a signed-in tab answers only with its token; signing out in one tab returns every other tab to sign-in. · fulfils AC-299d

## The shell

- **SC-894** — The menu has three parts — Dashboard and HQ on top, every site in the middle, the jobs (Maintenance, Backups, Activity, Fleet admins, Help) pinned below; each site is named after its machine, HQ's own first then by name, with its state in words beside its dot, and HQ's page and HQ's own site's page are two entries; Find a site filters by the machine's or the site's name; HQ needs attention when it does not answer; pages change without a reload and draw no breadcrumb trail; the older addresses lead to their pages; the menu folds to icons and the browser remembers it; every page says it is served to this machine alone; the light or dark choice is applied before the first paint and remembered. · fulfils AC-300c
- **SC-895** — The person signed in is named by their username, initials and role in their menu, which opens Your account, switches light and dark, and signs out (ending the session on the server); Your account says since when they hold the role and sends them to HQ to change their name and password, going without what HQ has not said. · fulfils AC-299a

## The Dashboard

- **SC-896** — The Dashboard puts what needs the fleet admin on top, then the fleet with HQ as its hub — its card titled with the organisation's name, giving HQ's address and opening it in a new tab — and every site under it in the menu's order, each in one line: what it is, its runs, its disk, and a planned change or an overdue backup; then the totals: agents running against capacity, the next maintenance, and how many machines keep backups off their machine. Past twenty sites they are a compact list with a search; with only HQ's machine it invites a second site; with no machine it says so; when HQ does not answer, that needs the fleet admin first and the fleet shows what was last known; it reads again every 15 seconds and when the window comes back to the front. A card is one link to its page. · fulfils AC-300a
- **SC-897** — A machine's state is said in words: Healthy; starting, never needing attention; needing attention for a part stopped or failing past its start, a disk or memory 90 % full or more, protections that do not hold, or an install the host service says is not healthy (in its own sentence); online before its first figures, waiting; silent for 10 minutes and ticked to be told, Not answering, needing attention; unticked, or silent for less, only Not heard from; HQ's machine always told; stopped on its machine, saying when, until it answers; left by itself, needing attention, outranking not answering. · fulfils AC-300b, AC-308a, AC-308b
- **SC-898** — A machine disconnected here stays on the Dashboard by its own name, saying who and when, with Add it again (leading to Add a site) and Remove from the list; a machine a restore replaced says so, names its site by the new machine, and can be removed from the list; neither card is a link. · fulfils AC-303a, AC-304b
- **SC-899** — A machine with no site reads "No site" under its own name, or "Waiting to receive the site" while a move to it waits, never Healthy, is not counted as a site and is not listed on Backups; its page is the machine's, with no action on a site, and says how to put a site there. · fulfils AC-300b

## A site's page and HQ's page

- **SC-900** — A site's page is named after its machine and tagged Site, with the site and then the machine in words; every action is a button in its header and none is about HQ; HQ's own site has the same page, tagged HQ's site, without what it cannot do on its own; the protections are a fact with no control, and say when they do not hold; Plan maintenance… opens the panel beside the page with the site chosen; Back up now opens its backups; a machine not answering shows its last figures as old, and why; without figures it says so; an unknown machine says so. · fulfils AC-300c
- **SC-901** — Notify… on a site's page shows the machine's setting to tell the fleet admins if it stops answering, changes it and reads the machine again, and a refused change is put back saying why; Add a site and a restore ask the same, ticked to begin with, and a restore's warning says when the lost machine was unticked. · fulfils AC-308a
- **SC-902** — HQ's page is HQ, then the machine it runs on, every action a button in its header; it says how many sites are connected, its own included; with no backup location it says what is at stake and where to set one; it shows the front door and its renewal trouble in one line; Plan maintenance… opens the panel with HQ chosen and Back up now opens its backups; it leaves out what does not belong to HQ; without HQ's machine it says so. · fulfils AC-300c

## Adding and restoring a site

- **SC-903** — Add a site shows the code once, how long it works, and the command with HQ's address; when the machine arrives it shows the name and fingerprint to compare, and confirming starts the site step by step; it says when the site is connected and where HQ shows it; a refused machine is turned away and the code spent; an expired code can be made again; a site that could not be installed says why and offers trying again; cancelling withdraws the code. Restore from a backup is offered beside a new site. · fulfils AC-301a
- **SC-904** — Restore a lost site offers only a site whose machine is offline — never HQ's own site, a site that is moving, or one online — saying when it was last heard from and warning more strongly when that was recent; it warns before any code, then gives the command with the backup chosen on the new machine, never asking for the key; it shows the backup the machine restores and what it replaces before the fleet admin confirms, and says how it ended, marking the step that did not complete, in the host service's words when refused. · fulfils AC-304a

## Backups

- **SC-905** — The Backups screen lists HQ, then every site after its machine, with what each backup holds, when it was taken and whether a copy is elsewhere, and how many keep their backups on their own machine only; Restore a lost site… is at the top; a row opens that site's backups, with Back up now and Export… beside it; Back up now sends the backup command and says what the machine answered, a refusal in the host service's words. A site with no backup location shows the warning and the command to run on the machine — never a control; one with a location says when the last copy reached it and that the folder is shown only on the machine; an offline machine cannot be asked to back up or export. · fulfils AC-305a
- **SC-906** — Exporting takes a backup, exports that one and shows its key once — not drawn on the screen when kept as a file — and Done waits for the tick that the key is stored; the download unlocks then, hands the browser the file and says when the machine removed its copy; a stopped download says why and offers it again; an export no longer on the machine says so; a refused export says so in the machine's words and that the backup taken for it stays. · fulfils AC-305b

## Maintenance

- **SC-907** — The Maintenance page lists what is planned across the fleet, soonest first, and what was done in the last 30 days, each named for what it does, where, and who planned it, taking each change at its newest notice and keeping the same id on two machines as two changes; it says when nothing is planned; it cancels planned maintenance on the machine that holds it after asking, changes its time, and shows the machine's own refusal in the dialog. An upgrade is followed step by step only as far as its notices say, ending on its health check or its failure. · fulfils AC-306a, AC-306c
- **SC-908** — Plan maintenance opens beside the page: Which lists HQ, then every site after its machine, searchable; What swaps its form below, keeping Which, what was typed in each form, and When; changing Which keeps What when the new thing allows it, and says why when not; the button's words follow When, Suspend's in the danger kind; an answer that arrives while another form is shown is there on coming back; Escape, the close button and a click beside it close it. It sends an upgrade, capacity change, suspend or move to the machine that holds it, now or scheduled; a site that is not answering says why nothing can reach it and offers no button; a capacity form starts from the current ceilings and refuses what is not valid beside its field. · fulfils AC-306a
- **SC-909** — When is Now or a time: a time must leave room for the members' notice — at least 5 minutes from now — or it is refused with that sentence beside it; the time offered to begin with is never in the past or inside that lead; the panel says in one line what Now does and what members see of a time; a change is sent as scheduled only when it has a time. · fulfils AC-306b
- **SC-910** — HQ is suspended from Plan maintenance, now or at a time, and reads as suspended once its machine goes quiet after it was asked, since the answer cannot come back through HQ; a suspended HQ's page says since when, offers Resume HQ, and the command on the machine for when it cannot reach it; an upgrade asked now says HQ restarts and ends when HQ answers on the new version. · fulfils AC-307a
- **SC-911** — Erasing HQ is confirmed only with HQ's address, or its host name, typed in, and sends the address; on a date it is a scheduled change with the countdown everyone sees; a refusal stays in the dialog in the host service's words; HQ's page counts the days of the grace period left. · fulfils AC-307b

## Moves and disconnecting

- **SC-912** — Move site… scheduled for a time shows the code for the new machine, then its name and fingerprint to confirm; started from Plan maintenance it is followed on its own page, with the code until a machine uses it; a move to a machine already connected offers only those with no site and no move and shows no code; its steps follow only what the machines said, ending only on what the Fleet Console says; a scheduled move can be cancelled; a finished move's notice opens its outcome, or the site's page once the outcome is no longer held. · fulfils AC-302a, AC-302c
- **SC-913** — Move HQ… to a new machine and a new address says what follows HQ — connected machines are told, offline ones need pointing — then gives the new machine's code; an HQ with its own domain keeps it and no address is asked for; a new address on the same machine is changed now by the host service; a move under way opens on its steps, and once done the offline machines get their commands; a failed move says HQ stays where it was. · fulfils AC-302b
- **SC-914** — Disconnect… asks first, says what stays on the machine, and needs the machine's name typed; cancelling sends nothing; a machine that left by itself is removed from the list instead. · fulfils AC-303a

## HQ's address and front door

- **SC-915** — The front door line says whether HQ is served by the machine admin's own web server (the default) or the host service's, with its certificates or its renewal trouble, and whether uploaded files have a host of their own; turning the host service's front door on warns first, with No the default, and a refusal while a port is in use offers Try again and Keep my own web server; Change… opens Plan maintenance on HQ with Move chosen. · fulfils AC-313a

## Notices, Activity, Fleet admins, Help

- **SC-916** — The notices panel opens beside the menu from the bell, kept in the address without a new history entry, and closes on Escape, its close button, a click outside, or a change of page; each notice opens what it is about; Dismiss puts one away in HQ and the count falls, one that clears itself has no Dismiss and says so, Dismiss all asks first and empties the bell of all but those, and HQ's refusal shows beside the notice; with nothing it says nothing needs you. · fulfils AC-309a
- **SC-917** — Activity is the record, newest first, of who did what to what, kept in HQ for a year where nobody can edit it, worded in sentences — never the name the record keeps — and naming a machine admin at the command line when no account did it; it shows no notices and has no Dismiss; it finds by who, by HQ or a site, and by kind; it reads the next page when asked, and says when HQ is older than the record. · fulfils AC-309b
- **SC-918** — Fleet admins lists who holds the role, and says who can give it and that every grant is announced; it gives the role to an HQ account found by its username, saying when there is no such account or it already holds the role; it removes the role after saying what happens to them, shows HQ's refusal in the dialog, does not let the last fleet admin remove themselves and says what to do instead, and says when HQ is older than finding an account. · fulfils AC-310a
- **SC-919** — Help lists the guide's pages for running Agenthix, a card for each topic, and every topic opens as a page with its title and headings, its commands as code and its tables as tables; links between topics open inside Help, a link to a heading carries the section, every link goes to a topic and heading that exist or to an `https` address opened in a new tab without the opener, and a page Help does not carry shows only its words; HTML in a page is shown as words, no image is loaded, so Help makes no request elsewhere: a picture at an `https` address shows as its alt text, a link opening the picture in a new tab, and any other picture is left out, and nothing raw passes through. Each screen has a How this works link to its part of the guide. · fulfils AC-311a

## Messages never in the content (added 2026-11-11)

Written at the close of the unit *a softer, more polished design system*, from the finished code; the shared pieces are in [`ui.md`](ui.md) (**SC-1906**, **SC-1907**).

- **SC-1909** — The Fleet Console's screens say messages as hq does: a press that failed is the attention dialog, opening once with its words, its title, *Try again* and its own action, and again only for new words, and a read's failure is not the dialog but a quiet retry and a line (**SC-1946**); an option of a drop-down that cannot be chosen is greyed with the reason on hover, and a press on it opens the dialog without choosing it; a whole drop-down refused stays focusable, and a press says why instead of opening its list. *(Changed 2026-11-11.)* · fulfils AC-457a, AC-457b
- **SC-1946** — A read the Fleet Console's screens make on their own (the dashboard, a site's page, address and front door, maintenance, backups, activity, Cloud's connection, live previews) is retried quietly (**SC-1940**) and shows nothing while it runs or when one try works; when the retries are spent with nothing on screen the line takes the place of the content with *Try again*, with figures on screen they stay and the meta line says *Not up to date · read HH:MM* a minute on, never the dialog. A read of the Fleet Console says why in its own words — the Fleet Console, or Cloud, never *HQ did not answer* when it was not HQ — and a final answer is not retried. A join whose first poll fails keeps its code and says it in the meta line; a press that fails, such as *Show earlier* in Activity or *Cancel* on Add a site, is the dialog with *Try again*, and only a refused value is a field error under the six digits. · fulfils AC-457a, AC-464a, AC-464b
