# Scenarios — `ui/` (`@agenthix/ui`)

> Module contract: what `@agenthix/ui` promises to its consumer, `hq/` (`hq/src/`); `site/` does not depend on it.
> The agent proposes changes (additions, rewordings, removals); a human reviews and approves — see [`../README.md`](../README.md).
> Exercised through consumers — no in-repo test runner.

---

## Theming + tokens

- Design tokens cascade from `theme/tokens.css` to consumers.
- Light is the default theme, with no attribute on the root element; `data-theme="dark"` on the root applies dark, and `data-theme="light"` selects light explicitly. Both are defined for every token.
- Component files contain no hex literals — all colours go through tokens.

## Brand

- `Logomark` renders SVG at the expected size and colour token.
- `Wordmark` renders SVG with the correct font + colour.

## Primitives

- `Button` variants (primary / secondary / ghost) render with token-driven styles.
- `InlineEdit` opens on focus, commits on blur, cancels on Escape.
- `NativeSelect` renders + emits change events through the standard form interface.
- `StatusBadge` renders a task's status as a label, with its colours from tokens.
- `PlaceAvatar` draws a project's initial, or a board mark, on a colour derived from the item's id, at the size asked for.
- `PanelItem` draws an item of the Notifications and Runs panels in two lines with its time, one action and, where given, a ✕; the whole item is tappable, and its action and ✕ do not open it. `PanelGroupHeading` heads a group with its count.
- `ViewHeaderRow` lets a view inside a tools panel replace the panel's header row with its own, so the panel keeps one header row.
- `SearchField` and `FilterSelect` are the search box and filter used alike by the conversations list and the Tasks tool.

## Domain components

- `ProjectCard` renders name + progress bar + completion percentage.
- `TaskRow` renders title + status pill + assignee.
- `RunStateBadge` shows a task's run state with one label wherever it appears — Queued, Throttled, Running, Needs you or Run failed — and nothing when no run is pending or the value is unknown; it takes its colours from tokens.
- ~~`AutomationSection` presents a boundary's automation: its own choices beside the organisation's values, any value limited by the organisation marked as such, the last round and what it started, and the next round's earliest time; it is read-only for a person who cannot change it.~~ **Retired 2026-10-07** — the component is gone; a place's automation is shown by the Automation tool (below, and **SC-511**, **SC-512** in [`hq.md`](hq.md)).
- `AutomationOverview`, `AutomationRoutines` and `AutomationRules` present a place's Automation tool: its status with Pause or Resume, what runs and what is stuck now, the Routines and Rules views and what starts later; each is read-only for a person who cannot change it.
- `TasksList`, `TasksToolHeader`, `TaskViews`, `TaskInTool` and `TaskSessionView` present the Tasks tool: the three groups, Backlog and Done, a task with what waits and its comments, and its session with each run as a divider; one layout narrow and one wide, chosen by the width they are given.
- `RoutineForm` presents the form that creates or edits a routine, with the schedule fields shared with the task's start-time editor (`TaskScheduleEditor`); `RoutineSentence`, `RoutineNextRound` and `RoutineFacts` present what a routine does and its next round, `RoutineRoundList` its rounds newest first, and `RoutineSquares` its last rounds at a glance.
- `KanbanColumns` (through `BoardColumnsView`) lets a card be dragged from one column to another, except a card whose run is active.

## Files: the frame and its renderers (added 2026-10-27)

A file is drawn only by a renderer behind one boundary: it is given the file's facts and a way to read its bytes through the relay and reports one state, and the frame draws everything around it. `@agenthix/ui/files` holds the registry, the contract, the frame and the renderers.

- **SC-1578** — The frame words eight states: (1) no renderer for the kind; (2) past the renderer's cap with part shown, the renderer under a line saying so; (3) past the cap with nothing useful to show; (4) looks like text but is not, by its name or by the renderer's report; (5) a PDF with no files address; (6) changed while open, where *Show the new version* opens it again; (7) the site's relay is not answering, naming the site and the relay from the answer, with *Try again* and Download greyed; (8) the site is not answering, or, for a past version of a file, the site cannot read it. Download is a link in every state but 7 and 8. An empty file (**SC-1884**), a hard-linked file (**SC-1885**) and a file that is not there have states of their own. *(Changed 2026-11-10)* · fulfils AC-390c
- **SC-1579** — Around the renderer the frame says *Loading…* while the answer is asked for, draws the breadcrumb, the title and a facts line with the kind's own fact (code by its language), draws the one header (**SC-1893**) with *Full screen* and ⋯ as the caller gives them (*Copy link*, *Copy path*, *Show in Files* and *Mention*), says a confirmation under the actions and never in the facts line, and mounts the renderer afresh for a new version, aborting the old one's reads. · fulfils AC-390a
- **SC-1580** — The frame never makes an `href` of a link that is not an http(s) address or a path. · fulfils AC-392d
- **SC-1581** — An embedded file is drawn as a card for a kind whose renderer does not embed, which opens it on a click, and with no controls for a renderer that embeds; the card says why when the file cannot be shown. · fulfils AC-392c
- **SC-1582** — A renderer reads bytes through the relay link: the first bytes with a `Range` request and no credentials (whole when the limit covers the file, stopping at the limit when the relay ignores the range, an empty file whole), a range by its inclusive offsets (cut out of a whole answer), a 409 as *changed*, a network failure or a refusal as *unavailable*, the abort signal passed on with an aborted read staying an abort, and a limit or range that means nothing refused. · fulfils AC-390c
- **SC-1583** — Text is drawn wrapped, as text and never as HTML, split into lines (**SC-1891**), with its line count (one line for a file with no newline, none for an empty file, which says it is empty, **SC-1884**); it reads at most 1 MB and shows the first 1 MB of a larger file under a line saying so; a multi-byte character cut at the cap is not called bytes that are not text; bytes that are not text are said in words, a 409 is the changed state and an unavailable relay is the relay state. · fulfils AC-390b
- **SC-1584** — Markdown is drawn as a page through the caller's Markdown renderer with its line count, switching to *Source* — the text as written, never as Markdown or HTML — and back; it reads at most 1 MB and reports partial past it, says bytes that are not text in words, turns a 409 into the changed state, embedded is a card since only images draw inside a page, and without a caller's renderer the text is drawn as text. · fulfils AC-390b
- **SC-1585** — Code is highlighted as spans of text with line numbers and its language named in the facts line, with colours taken from the design tokens and no hex value on the page; *Wrap* is off by default and can be turned on, and is on from the start in a narrow view (**SC-1890**); a language the highlighter lacks is drawn with line numbers and no colour; a file past 512 KB is drawn plain with a line saying why and all of it is shown up to 1 MB; it reads at most 1 MB and says so for a larger file; bytes that are not text and a changed file are said in words. · fulfils AC-390b
- **SC-1586** — JSON is drawn as a tree folded below the first level with its fact in the facts line, each fold opened and closed from a keyboard-reachable button, *Text* showing the file as written and *Tree* going back; every value is drawn as text, never as HTML; invalid JSON is drawn as text with the line of the problem (**SC-1889**); it reads at most 5 MB, and a larger file is drawn as the whole records in its first 1 MB (**SC-1888**); a long array shows 200 children at a time. · fulfils AC-390b
- **SC-1587** — CSV is read to RFC 4180 — quotes, escaped quotes, commas and newlines inside fields, CRLF, a lone CR, a missing final newline, a byte order mark, blank lines in the middle kept as empty rows, an unterminated quote read to the end — and the first rows past the cap are kept and all counted. · fulfils AC-390b
- **SC-1588** — A table is drawn with a header, row numbers and the cells, the row count in the facts line, sorted by a column (numbers as numbers), then reversed, then back to the file order; every cell is drawn as text; 10,000 rows of a larger table are shown with a line saying how many there are, and then it cannot be sorted (**SC-1887**); it reads at most 5 MB, dropping the row the cut fell in and reporting the part shown; it scrolls sideways inside its own area and keeps its columns' widths (**SC-1887**). · fulfils AC-390b
- **SC-1589** — An image is drawn from its preview link with no referrer, with its pixel size in the facts line once loaded (left out when an SVG has none), fitted to the area with *Fit* / *100%* to switch (at 100% at its real size, **SC-1892**), on a checkered ground made from a token; an image that will not load is said unreadable, a file over 20 MB is not loaded and said too large, and embedded it has no controls and is at most 480 px tall. · fulfils AC-390b
- **SC-1590** — A PDF is the registry's entry for `pdf`: framed, up to 50 MB, a card when embedded; the viewer page on the files address is framed sandboxed with the least it needs and the ticket in the address fragment (the page's theme told to it); the page count and reports come from the viewer on the files origin and messages from any other origin, window or shape are ignored; a PDF it cannot read is said in words for a PDF, one page is said as one page, a PDF past 50 MB is said too large and nothing is framed, nothing is framed for a link that is not a ticket on an http(s) files address, and with no files address the frame's state 5 applies. · fulfils AC-390b

## The design system's states (added 2026-11-11)

Written at the close of the unit *a softer, more polished design system*, from the finished code. Each state is drawn by one shared primitive or class in `ui/src/primitives` (`controlStyles.ts`, `buttonStyles.ts`), never by a look-alike; the rules are `ui/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`'s. A card's states are **SC-799**, corrected above. How hq says its messages with these pieces is in [`hq.md`](hq.md) (**SC-1910** to **SC-1912**).

- **SC-1900** — `Button`, in every variant (primary, secondary, ghost, danger), has a hover step and a pressed step, neither while disabled: the main button turns a deeper indigo and deeper again pressed, a secondary button's fill goes one tone step and its edge firms up, a ghost button's text goes to full ink on a tone step. Keyboard focus is a 2 px accent outline 2 px outside the edge, for the keyboard only and never on a click, drawn just inside the edge for a row in a menu or a scrolling list. A disabled control is faded to 40 % with no shadow and no hover, and a control refused through `aria-disabled` looks the same, stays focusable and drops its hover and pressed steps. The looks a link or a bare element borrows (`BUTTON_PRIMARY` and the others) are the strings `Button` draws. · fulfils AC-456b, AC-456c, AC-456d
- **SC-1901** — A field (`Input`, `Select`, `Textarea`, `NativeSelect`, `DateField`, `TimeField`, `SearchField`, `FilterSelect`, `InlineEdit`'s editors) is drawn on the field token with its hairline — white in light, one step up in dark, never a grey field on a white page and never a fill of its own: its edge firms up on hover, and focused its edge darkens with a faint halo, never the accent; disabled, it is at 40 % with no hover. A box that holds a field (the Markdown box, the composer) takes the focus of the field inside it the same way. A picker in a toolbar or a menu (`PlainSelect`, Sort, ＋ Filter) is plain text with no box and no field fill. · fulfils AC-456b
- **SC-1902** — `SegmentedControl` is one of a small set, as a radio group or a tab list, named by its label or by one elsewhere: the chosen piece is raised (white in light, one step up in dark) with a faint lift and ink text in medium weight, the others plain secondary text with no box. It takes one Tab stop, on the chosen piece; the arrow keys move the choice and the focus and wrap round, Home and End go to the ends, a disabled piece is passed over, and disabled as a whole it is at 40 % with `aria-disabled` and answers nothing. A piece refused for a reason is greyed but not disabled and says why on focus; the arrow keys reach it but only move the focus, and a press opens the dialog without choosing it. `FilterTabs` is drawn as the same strip, the chosen tab pressed. · fulfils AC-456a, AC-456b
- **SC-1903** — `Switch` is a switch named by its label that says whether it is on: indigo when on, a hairline outline when off. A click, Space or Enter turns it; disabled it is at 40 % and changes nothing; on a phone its hit area grows to 44 px tall; refused, it stays focusable, gives the reason, and a press opens the dialog without turning it. · fulfils AC-456a, AC-456d
- **SC-1904** — A row in a menu or a list is secondary text with a muted icon, one tone step and full ink on hover, one more step pressed; the chosen row has the selected grey fill, ink text and icon, in medium weight, never an indigo tint. A menu's chosen item (a task's current agent, Sort's chosen order) is a `menuitemradio` marked checked, with an ink check; a menu item reached by the keyboard is ringed inside the menu, and choosing one gives the focus back to the button that opened it. · fulfils AC-456a, AC-456b

- **SC-1906** — `AttentionDialog` opens in the centre of the screen as a dialog with a title (*That did not work* when none is given), the words (rich text allowed, announced as an alert), Close, the caller's own actions, each closing the dialog before it runs, and *Try again* where there is a retry; the page keeps its layout. - **SC-1907** — A refusal known in advance (`Button`'s `refusal`, `useRefusal` on any control, `Refused` round a switch, a set of checkboxes or a select, a refused piece of a segmented control, a switch, an item of a task's ⋯ menu): the control is greyed but not disabled, so it stays focusable and says it is unavailable; the reason is its accessible description, and its tooltip on hover and on keyboard focus, wrapping when longer than a name; a press, Enter or Space opens the attention dialog with the full explanation (the reason when there is no more, titled *Not available now* unless a title is given) and the refusal's own actions, and never runs the control's action or submits its form. Without a refusal the control is drawn and works as usual, and a menu's other items keep working. · fulfils AC-457b
- **SC-1908** — A row that opens something is reached from the keyboard: a session row in the activity list is a Tab stop named *Open <agent>'s session: <task>*, opened with Enter or Space and drawn with the focus ring, its own buttons keeping their keys; an editable section of an agent's instructions, while nothing is being edited, is a Tab stop named *Edit <label>* that Enter opens for editing, and a read-only section is not a button. · fulfils AC-463a

## Messages by kind (added 2026-11-11)

The hq screens that use these pieces are in [`hq.md`](hq.md) (**SC-1929** to **SC-1939**).

- **SC-1940** — The quiet retry is one policy for every read a screen makes on its own: up to three retries after the first failure, at 1, 2 and 4 seconds each moved by up to 20 % so tabs do not retry together, none for a final answer (400, 401, 403, 404, 409, 422) or for a site that did not answer, a hidden tab waiting until it is shown again, and nothing shown while it runs. A hand-written read or one turn of a poll takes the same policy, stopping when asked to. · fulfils AC-464a
- **SC-1941** — `useBackgroundRead` decides what a failed read says: nothing during the retries, with or without data; after them with nothing on screen, a nudge in place of the content — *<what> could not be read*, why and *Try again*, or a final answer in its own words with no *Try again*; with data on screen, nothing until it is a minute old (two poll intervals when longer) and then *Not up to date · read HH:MM · Try again* for the meta line; the next good read clears all three, and while the frame line says HQ is not answering a held-data nudge is not drawn. · fulfils AC-464a, AC-464b, AC-464c
- **SC-1942** — A nudge is one quiet line a screen reader hears without being interrupted, its action inline and whole, wrapping as one sentence; it draws nothing without words, may keep a reserved slot of the same height, sits inline in a row, is indented after a thread, and in place of content says what could not be shown, why and *Try again*. It never moves a control: a screen's nudge takes a slot that is there before it is needed, so the timeline's chart does not move when its runs-not-read line comes. The frame line is drawn only when given words, over the room under the screen bar. · fulfils AC-464a, AC-465a
- **SC-1943** — A toast says a few words, in a polite region with no buttons, for what worked and the screen does not show (*Copied*, *Invitation sent*); it goes by itself after four seconds, shows one at a time (a new one replaces the last and starts its own time), waits while hovered or focused, and sits above the bottom by the offset the frame gives. It is never used for an error or for anything to act on. · fulfils AC-467c
- **SC-1944** — A form field's value that is refused is said under the field as a status and the text stays; any other failure of the same save (an inline edit's) is the dialog with *Try again*, which tries the save again. · fulfils AC-457c, AC-467a
- **SC-1945** — `ConfirmDialog` asks with Cancel and an action named for what it does, holds the action while busy and draws nothing when closed; it is the one confirm in `ui`, hq and the Fleet Console. · fulfils AC-467b

## Failures said in words (added 2026-11-14)

- **SC-2153** — `failureWords` says a failure for the server asked: no answer (the network, a timeout, or a gateway's 502, 503 or 504 with no words of the server's own) is *<Server> did not answer.*, the server's own 5xx with no words of its own is *Something went wrong in <Server>.*, and any answer the server wrote words for is those words; never the request's address or a status line. `FailureServer`, set once at an app's root (*HQ* in hq, *the Fleet Console* in the Fleet Console's screens), makes everything handed to `useAttentionWhen` and the nudges say it that way, the server's name starting a sentence with a capital; words and nodes are shown as given, even ones that look like a status line, and outside a `FailureServer` a failure keeps its message. · fulfils AC-494a, AC-494b · tests `ui/src/attention/__tests__/failureWords.test.ts`, `ui/src/attention/__tests__/failureServer.test.tsx`
