These pages cover the connections between **HQ**, your organisation's home in Agenthix, and what sits outside it: the
providers that supply models and voice, your own tools and GitHub. [How Agenthix works](../getting-started/how-agenthix-works.md) explains the parts they connect to.

1. [Providers](providers.md): where the organisation's models and voice come from, and how an organisation admin adds them.
2. [Models and the voice agents use](models.md): the defaults, an agent's own choices, and which models may run.
3. [Billing profiles and usage](billing-and-usage.md): who each project's AI cost belongs to, and what it was.
4. [Claude Code and other tools](connections.md): let a tool outside Agenthix work in HQ as you.
5. [Voice](phone-and-voice.md): talk to agents in a conversation.
6. [GitHub](github.md): where GitHub is connected, and what it is used for.
7. [Your settings](your-settings.md): what is yours alone to change.
