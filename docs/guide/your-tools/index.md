These pages cover the connections between **HQ**, your organisation's home in Agenthix, and what sits outside it: the
models that answer, your own tools, voice and GitHub. [How Agenthix works](../getting-started/how-agenthix-works.md) explains the parts they connect to.

1. [Models: the organisation's and your own](models.md): who can give agents a model, and which one a run uses.
2. [Claude Code and other tools](connections.md): let a tool outside Agenthix work in HQ as you.
3. [Voice](phone-and-voice.md): set up speech, and talk to agents in a conversation.
4. [GitHub](github.md): back a project's files up to a repository.
5. [Your settings](your-settings.md): what is yours alone to change.
