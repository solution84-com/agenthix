**Your settings**, in the menu behind your name at the foot of the side bar, is only yours. What belongs to the whole
organisation is under **Manage**, in the same menu, for organisation admins. [The organisation's page and the admin
area](../people/your-organisation.md) describes both.

![Settings, with its sections down to Devices and connections](https://github.com/solution84-com/agenthix/releases/download/v0.1.0-alpha.4/settings.png)

## Profile

At the top, you can edit your name and use **Add a picture** or **Change picture**. **Handle** shows your username,
which you sign in with. **Your time zone** begins as **Use my browser's time zone**; pick another to
see times in it.

## Appearance

Choose **Light**, which is the default, or **Dark**. It is remembered in this browser.

## Conversations

Under **Conversations**, choose what happens when you send a message while an agent is still answering:

- **Wait:** your message waits for the answer to finish, then the agent replies to it.
- **Interrupt:** the agent stops the answer it was giving and starts on your new message instead.

## Voice

**Adaptive voice**, on to begin with, covers what an agent adds to a voice conversation beyond speaking its reply:
listening sounds ("mm-hm") while you talk, and, when a reply will take a while, an opening sentence from the agent
("Let me look into that"), or a stored standard phrase if it cannot give one. Turn it off and the agent says nothing
until its reply starts. Either way, the agent notices when you have finished and tells your voice from its own. The
**(i)** beside it explains the same. A change applies from the next time you start voice.

**Voice sounds**, on to begin with, are the short sounds a call makes: when voice is ready and when it ends, when you
mute or unmute, and when the connection drops. Turn them off and a call makes no sounds of its own; you still hear the
agent.

## Primary agent

The agent a new conversation starts with, **Kernel** to begin with. You can still pick another agent before you send the
first message.

## Your Claude subscription

Your own Claude login, used only for work in your own place: your space and your personal projects. **Sign in with
Claude** to add it, **Sign out** to remove it. See [Models](models.md#your-claude-subscription).

## Which models may run for you

A list that narrows the organisation's, everywhere you work: choose **Any model, except these** or **Only these**, and
add models or patterns. It never widens what the organisation allows. See
[Which models may run](models.md#which-models-may-run). You can still change a conversation's model from its message
box at any time.

## Memory

What agents remember about you, and what they remember where you work. Select **Manage** to open it: you choose a set,
then add, edit, pin or delete memories. What is about you is yours to change, and nobody else can read it. Each agent's
page also has a **Memory** section. [Instructions and memory](../agents/instructions-and-memory.md)
covers how memory works.

## Devices and connections

- **Connections:** **Manage** lists the tools that act for you, such as your own Claude Code. See
  [Claude Code and other tools](connections.md).

## Sign-in

Under **Password**, select **Change**, then enter your **Current password**, your **New password** (at least 8
characters) and **New password again**, and select **Change password**. You stay signed in here; everywhere else
is signed out. **Sign out**, in the same menu behind your name, ends this browser's session.

If you forget your password, an administrator makes you a link, as
[When someone forgets their password or leaves](../people/passwords-and-leaving.md) describes.
