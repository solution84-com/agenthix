These pages use a project on **HQ**, your organisation's home in Agenthix, and the **Execution Site** that runs its agents: see [How Agenthix works](../getting-started/how-agenthix-works.md). If you haven't made a task yet, start with [Your first task](../getting-started/first-task.md).

1. [Projects, and who can join](projects.md): open and private projects, roles, members and settings.
2. [Boards, tasks and the timeline](boards-and-tasks.md): plan work, move it along and see it over time.
3. [Follow what agents are doing](runs-and-approvals.md): the Runs screen, Notifications and answering an approval.
4. [Files, workspaces and previews](files-and-previews.md): what a project keeps, and the apps agents build in it.
5. [Knowledge](knowledge.md): the processes, decisions and reference a place shares, as pages that people and agents search, edit and restore.
6. [Open and download files](open-and-download-files.md): read what agents made in place, copy its link, and what to do when a file can't be opened.
7. [A task's files](task-files.md): attachments, working files and links to them.
8. [Files in conversations](files-in-conversations.md): chips, Mention and `@`, and how agents point at files and tasks.
9. [Images in pages](images-in-pages.md): show a picture in a knowledge page, a task or a reply.
