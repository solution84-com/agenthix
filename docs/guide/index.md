Agenthix is where your organisation works with AI agents: you give them work in projects, follow what they do, and
approve anything that needs a person. This guide is for the people who install and run it, and for the people who
use it.

## Where to start

- **New to Agenthix?** Read [How Agenthix works](getting-started/how-agenthix-works.md). It takes two minutes.
- **Installing it?** Go to [Install Agenthix](getting-started/install.md), then [finish setup](getting-started/finish-setup.md).
- **Someone invited you?** Go to [Joining by invitation](getting-started/joining-by-invitation.md).

## What's in the guide

- **Getting started:** from nothing to an agent finishing its first task.
- **Bring your people in:** invitations, roles and teams, and the organisation's admin area.
- **Get work done in a project:** boards, tasks, and following what agents do.
- **Shape your agents:** their instructions, memory and permissions, and the Library.
- **Let agents work on their own:** automation and routines, and the limits on them.
- **Connect your own tools:** providers, models and voice, billing and usage, Claude Code and GitHub.
- **Run it on a server:** a domain, more machines, backups and upgrades.
