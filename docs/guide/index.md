Agenthix is where your organisation works with AI agents: you give them work in projects, follow what they do, and
approve anything that needs a person. This guide is for the people who install and run it, and for the people who
use it.

## Where to start

- **New to Agenthix?** Read [How Agenthix works](getting-started/how-agenthix-works.md). It takes two minutes.
- **Installing it?** Go to [Install Agenthix](getting-started/install.md), then [set up your HQ](getting-started/setup-hq.md).
- **Someone invited you?** Go to [Joining by invitation](getting-started/joining-by-invitation.md).

## What's in the guide

- **Getting started:** from nothing to an agent finishing its first task.
- **Bring your people in:** invitations, roles and teams, and the organisation's admin area.
- **Get work done in a project:** tasks and boards, sprints, starting work, the Workroom, and opening the files agents make.
- **Shape your agents:** their instructions, memory and permissions, and the Library.
- **Give agents skills:** find, add, give, write and review the skills agents follow, and see when one was used.
- **Let agents work on their own:** routines on a schedule, and the limits on what agents may start.
- **Keep secrets and connect GitHub:** workflows that use a secret so agents never hold one, and GitHub through them.
- **Connect your own tools:** providers, models and voice, billing and usage, Claude Code and GitHub.
- **Run it on a server:** a domain, more machines, backups and upgrades.
- **Looking for a word?** The [Glossary](glossary.md) lists every word Agenthix shows, where to find it and what else it is called.
- **Something not working, or not there yet?** The [open issues](https://github.com/solution84-com/agenthix/issues) on
  GitHub list what is known, and you can report what isn't.
