The quickest way to see an agent at work is to talk to one. Every new HQ — your organisation's home in Agenthix —
comes with a built-in agent, **Kernel**, which knows how Agenthix works and can help you set up the rest.

## Start from Home

![Home, with the greeting, the message box and three ways to begin](screenshot:home)

**Home** is the first thing you see after signing in. It shows what needs you, what's running and what's coming up
today, and lets you start a conversation straight away:

1. Choose an agent in the box under **What would you like to work on?** — **Kernel**, to begin with.
2. Type your message where it says **Ask any agent…**, then press Enter or select **Send**.

Home also offers three ways to begin with Kernel: **What can you do?**, **Set up my first project** and **Invite my
team**. Select one to start that conversation in the conversation beside the page; Home stays where it is.

If Home says **Agents can't answer you yet: no model access is set up**, follow its link to add one: see
[Models](../your-tools/models.md).

## The conversation

![A conversation with Kernel in Chat](screenshot:chat-conversation)

Your conversation opens beside the page, on the right of the screen, where it stays as you move around HQ. It belongs
to **Your space**, your own private place, outside any project. The title and the agent's name are at the top: the agent names the conversation in a few words after its first
reply, and until then it is **New conversation**. The agent answers as soon as it can; keep writing in the message box to reply, and select **Stop** (the button
at the right of the box while it answers) to stop it. A message you send while the agent is still answering waits behind
its reply, marked as waiting, with **Send now**, which stops the reply to answer you first, and **Cancel**; your
[Settings](../your-tools/your-settings.md#conversations) choose whether a message waits or interrupts.

- **See what it looked at:** when an agent looks something up to answer, a small grey pill appears beside its name:
  **Thinking · 6 s**, with a pulsing dot, while it works, and **Thought for 6 s** after. Select it to see each step it
  took and how long it took. If the agent started work, the pill says so: **Thought for 6 s · started 2**.
- **Copy a reply:** select **Copy** under it.
- **Change the model:** select the **plus** at the left of the message box, then choose under **Model**.
- **Before the first message,** the box also lets you choose the agent. After it, a conversation keeps its agent; to
  talk to another, start a new conversation.
- **The menu** (⋯) at the top holds **Summarise now**, **Keep out of archiving** and **Show its runs**. **Delete** is
  beside it for the person who started the conversation. A long conversation is summarised for you as it grows: see
  [Long conversations](#long-conversations).

Your conversations in **Your space** are listed in **Chat**, newest first. Select one to open it beside the page. A
conversation nobody has written in for 90 days is archived, not deleted, unless you keep it out of archiving.

## The conversation beside your work

One conversation is open at a time, and it stays open wherever you go: another project, **Tasks**, **Settings**,
**Home**. Only you close or change it; moving to another page never does. The page and the conversation are both in the
address (the conversation as `?c=` followed by its number), so a reload, a link you share with yourself or Back brings
back both.

There is always a conversation beside the page. When none is open, such as when you have just signed in, HQ opens the
one you last wrote in, on this or any other device; if you have not written in one yet, it opens a new conversation in
**Your space**, ready for your first message. On a medium or narrow window it opens folded away, so the page shows first.

![A conversation docked on the right while the Tasks page is open](screenshot:docked-conversation)

A reply keeps arriving, and a voice call keeps going, while you move between pages. At the top of the conversation, a
small label says where it lives: **Your space**, a project or a task. Select it to open that place on the page. The
buttons beside it are:

- **Recent conversations** (the clock): your latest conversations in the place you are on, each with its place and when
  it was last written in. Select one to open it. **All conversations →** opens **Chat**.
- **New conversation** (＋): see [Start a new conversation](#start-a-new-conversation).
- **Expand over the page**: the conversation takes the whole width beside the side bar, for reading and writing at length.
  The same button, now **Dock beside the page**, puts it back. Moving to another page puts it back for you, so it never
  hides where you went.
- **Collapse (⌘J)**: the conversation shrinks to a narrow strip at the right edge. The strip shows the agent, a dot while
  work is under way, and the number of requests waiting for your okay. Select the strip, or press ⌘J (Ctrl+J on Windows
  and Linux) again, to open it. Nothing is lost while it is collapsed: the reply keeps arriving, and your unsent text is
  kept.

To make the conversation wider or narrower, drag the thin handle on its left edge; double-click the handle to return to
the usual width. With the handle selected, the left and right arrow keys change the width in steps. HQ remembers the
width on that browser. It is never narrower than the usual width, and never wider than half the window.

### On a smaller window

- **Wide windows** (1280 px and more) show the conversation beside the page.
- **Medium windows** (768 to 1279 px) slide the conversation over the page, and the side bar shows icons only from 1024 px
  (a place's sections are icons too, with their names when you point at them); below that it is behind the **☰** button. When you go to another page, the conversation
  folds to its strip, so you see where you went; select the strip to bring it back.
- **Narrow windows** (under 768 px, a phone) show a bar at the bottom of every screen with the agent, the
  conversation's title and a dot while work is under way. Select it to show the conversation full screen, and the back
  arrow at its top to return to the page.

Resizing the window never loses the conversation, its place in the thread or what you have typed.

## Start a new conversation

Select **＋** in the conversation, or **Talk about** a project (or **Talk in your space**) in the bar at the top of a
page. The conversation changes to a draft, headed **New conversation**, with **Cancel** at its right.

![A new conversation in the dock, with the places to choose from and the project already chosen](screenshot:new-conversation-draft)

The draft lists the places you can start in, under **You're here**, **Your space**, **Pinned** and **All projects**, with
a search box at the top (**Find a place…**). The place you are on is already chosen, and the message box is ready, so
usually you type and send. Only the chosen place shows a line about it: what it is and who can see what is said there.
Above the box, a line says which agent leads the conversation and what it can use. To start somewhere else, select
another place; searching narrows the list without moving your choice. The agent picker in the box lists the agents of the
place you chose.

Anything inside a project, such as a task, a board or a file, counts as that project: a conversation there is about the
whole project.

**Cancel**, or Esc, puts back exactly what was open before and throws away any text you had not sent. If nothing was
open, it folds the draft to its strip at the right edge, keeping what you typed. Sending turns the draft into the conversation, and the place becomes the label at its
top.

To talk instead of typing, select the voice button at the right of the empty box: the conversation starts in the
chosen place with the chosen agent, opens, and voice starts at once, so what you say is its first message. The button
is greyed when that agent cannot talk; select it to see why. See [Voice](../your-tools/phone-and-voice.md).

## Your space

**Your space** is in the side bar, under **Chat**. It is your own private place, and has the same sections as a project:
**Overview**, **Conversations**, **Tasks**, **Boards**, **Runs**, **Automation**, **Files**, **Artefacts**, **Knowledge**,
**Workspaces** and **Agents**. **Chat** shows only the list of its conversations. See
[Projects](../projects/projects.md#inside-a-project) for how a place's sections work.

## When the agent has more to do

The agent does not make you wait for longer work. It tells you it has started, in a line, and you can carry on talking.
Above the message box a bar shows what is going on: **4 working**, or **4 working · 2 need you**. Select it to open the
list. Each row says what the work is and where it is happening, how it is going, and has **Stop**. When the work is
done, a card appears in the conversation, **View results** opens what it did, and the agent tells you what it found.

If you ask for a task to start at a time, such as tomorrow at 9, the agent sets it and says so; nothing more comes back to
the conversation about it.

## Long conversations

When a conversation grows too long for the agent to read in full, the older part is folded into a summary. A line
marks where, with what the summary covers; select it to read the summary and then the messages it covers. **Summarise now**
does it at once. The agent keeps what mattered, such as decisions and anything you asked it to remember, and can look back
at the full summary or the exact words when it needs to.

## When an agent asks to do something

When the agent wants to do something its permissions leave to a person, such as running a command, it asks. The request
appears at the top of the bar above the message box, which opens by itself, as a row that says what the agent wants to
do, which work it belongs to and why.

- Select **Approve** to let it go on with what it asked for.
- Select **Decline** to refuse. You can say why (the box says *Say why, if you like*), and the agent reads it.

The agent does not speak when a request arrives; answer it in the bar. Only the person having the conversation can
answer in a personal or project conversation. In a task's conversation, anyone who works in the project can.

You decide what needs approval for each agent: see [Permissions and approvals](../agents/permissions.md).
