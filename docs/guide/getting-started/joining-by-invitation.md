Someone in your organisation has installed Agenthix and sent you an invitation link. The link opens your
organisation's **HQ** — its home in Agenthix — and lets you make your account.

## Accept the invitation

![Join your organisation, with who invited you and the fields for your account](https://github.com/solution84-com/agenthix/releases/download/v0.1.0/join-invitation.png)

The page says who invited you, with which role, and to which projects.

1. Fill in **Your name**, how you're shown on your work; **Username**, how you sign in; and a **Password** of at
   least 8 characters.
2. Select **Join** followed by your organisation's name, such as **Join Acme**.

If the page says the invitation **has expired**, **was withdrawn** or **has already been used**, ask the person who
sent it for a new one. If it says **This invitation link doesn't work**, check you copied the whole link.

## Set up your account

A few short steps follow:

1. **You:** add a **Picture** if you like, and check your **Time zone**. Agenthix uses it for times and schedules.
2. **Check everything before you start**, then select **Start working**. It lists what you are joining. The models
   are provided by your organisation, whose admins choose the ones everyone uses, so there is nothing to add.

You land in the first project you were invited to, or on **Home** if there was none.

## Find your way in

- **Home** shows what needs you and lets you start a conversation: see [Your first conversation](first-conversation.md).
- **Projects** are the shared places you were invited to: see [Your first task](first-task.md).
- **Your space** is your own private place, outside any project, and **Chat** lists your conversations in it.

## Signing in again

Sign in with your **Username** and **Password**. Agenthix keeps no email addresses, so it can't email you a
reset link: if you forget your password, ask an administrator for one. See
[When someone forgets their password or leaves](../people/passwords-and-leaving.md).
