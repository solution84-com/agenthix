An agent, a person, a team and the organisation each have a **picture** (the round avatar) and a **cover** (the wide
band across the top of their page). HQ draws them for you, so every one has a picture and a cover from the start, and
you can change either at any time, or upload your own.

## Change a picture or a cover

1. Open the page of the agent, the person, the team or the organisation. On an agent's, that is **Manage › Agents**,
   then **Manage** beside it.
2. Select **Change cover** on the cover, or **Change** beside the picture. A person can also open their picture full
   size by selecting it, and select **Change picture** there.
3. In the dialog that opens, choose **Avatar** or **Cover**, then **Generate** or **Upload** (below).
4. Select **Save**. It applies what you chose on both tabs. **Cancel** leaves everything as it was.

On the left, a preview card shows the cover and the picture together, marked **Preview · not saved yet**. The page
behind it changes only when you select **Save**.

Only an organisation administrator changes an agent's, a team's or the organisation's pictures. A person changes their
own, and an administrator can change anyone's.

## Generate a picture

The **Avatar** tab opens on a gallery of looks, each drawn on this agent's, person's or team's own name, in groups:

| Group | What it holds | Offered to |
|---|---|---|
| **Robots** | Glossy robots, many holding something that suggests a job | Agents |
| **Faces** | Drawn characters and pixel faces, and the same without a body | Agents and people |
| **Symbols** | Icons, shapes, glass, rings and identicons | The organisation |
| **Initials** | The letters of the name on a coloured background | Everyone |

Select a look to fine-tune it. Each look has its own choices, such as hair, eyes, clothing or the robot, and every
one has a **Background** colour. **Zoom** moves the picture closer or further away. **Mirror** flips it. **Surprise me**
draws another at random, and **Start again** goes back to where you began. **← All looks** returns to the gallery. A
look already saved is marked **Current**.

If a name changes, Initials are drawn again from the new name.

The **Cover** tab has the cover's **Patterns**: waves, stripes, dots, rings and others, or **None** for a plain
colour. Choose a **Look** (**Soft**, **Vivid** or **Deep**), a **First colour** and a **Second colour**, and a
**Blend**: **One colour**, **Linear** or **Radial**. For a gradient, **Direction** turns it. **Another of this
pattern** draws the same pattern with a different arrangement.

## Upload your own

Select **Upload** in either tab, then drop a picture or **choose a file**. A square picture suits an avatar, which is
shown in a circle. A wide picture, about three times as wide as it is tall, suits a cover. The picture appears under a
frame of the shape it will be shown in. Drag it to move it, and use **Zoom**, the mouse wheel or a pinch to resize it.
**Reset** fits it again, and **Choose another** picks a different file.

Only the part in the frame is sent, made smaller in your browser first. Photos, PNG, GIF and WebP files work, and so
do HEIC photos where your browser can open them. A file that cannot be used is refused with a message. A drawing in
SVG is not accepted.

**Remove upload** drops an uploaded picture and brings back a generated one. **Generate** in the same tab does the
same: nothing is replaced until you select **Save**. A person's **Remove picture** goes back to their initials.

## What each one starts with

- **A new agent** gets a random robot on a random background, and a random cover. The **New Agent** dialog shows
  them, with **Change** to open the same dialog as above before the agent exists. If robots are not available in
  your installation, it gets a drawn character instead.
- **Kernel**, the built-in agent, has a robot of its own, with a halo and a bow tie. It is offered first in Kernel's
  gallery and to no other agent, so it always tells you that you are talking to Kernel.
- **A person** shows their initials until they choose a picture. They get a random cover when they join.
- **A team** gets its initials on a colour, and a random cover, when it is made.
- **The organisation** gets a generated symbol and a random cover when HQ is set up. Its picture is on its page and,
  if you choose to show it, on the sign-in page: see [Choose what the sign-in page shows](../people/your-organisation.md#choose-what-the-sign-in-page-shows).

A person's cover shows on their page and on their profile card, in **People**.

## Old pictures

When a picture or cover is replaced or removed, the old file is deleted from HQ's storage. Nothing else refers to it.
