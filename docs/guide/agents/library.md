The **Library** is a shelf of ready-made things for your organisation to start from. Using one makes your
organisation's own copy, which you can then change without touching the original.

Open **Overview** under your organisation's name in the menu behind your name at the foot of the side bar, and select its **Library** card. Anyone can look around. Adding things
to the organisation, and the buttons at the top, are for administrators: others see the buttons, but only an
administrator's click goes through.

## What's on the shelf

The Library's things are on four tabs, each with a count:

- **Agents**: select **Add to agents** beside one. Choose its **Handle**, then **Add to agents** again. It joins
  your organisation's agents, ready to [put to work in a project](create-an-agent.md#2-put-it-to-work-in-a-project).
- **Boards**: select **Use** to see a board's columns. You choose it when you add a board to a project, and the
  project gets its own copy.
- **Skills**: select **Use** to read a skill. An administrator can select **Install to Org** to copy it into the
  organisation's own skills, or **Edit** and **Delete** one made here, where the install button reads **Install to Org Active**. See [Skills](instructions-and-memory.md#skills).
- **Teams**: a team here is a whole group set up at once, with its agents and the projects and boards they work
  in. See below.

Each row says where it came from: **Built in** came with Agenthix, and **Made here** was made or imported by
your organisation. Use the search box to find one, and **Source** to show only one kind.

## Set up a team from the Library

Only an administrator can do this.

1. On the **Teams** tab, select **Use** beside a team, such as one for a small software team or for writing.
2. The page titled **Review:** and the team's name lists **Teams & Agents** and **Projects & Boards**. Untick what
   you don't want, and open a team to see its agents and their skills.
3. Each agent has a **handle** you can change. If one is already taken, Agenthix proposes another and marks the agent
   **handle collision**, with the original beside it.
4. Select **Apply Template**. Agenthix creates the teams, agents, projects and boards you left ticked, and takes you
   to **Organisation**.

## Make your own

As an administrator you also have **＋ New** at the top of the Library: **New board** and **New skill** make one
here; **New agent…** and **New team…** take you to where those are made. **Import** reads a package, a ZIP file, and
the **⋯** menu on a row has **Export**, which saves it as a package you can share.
