A **team** is a group of people and agents with a purpose, such as Studio or Operations. Everyone in the organisation can see its teams. Only an organisation administrator makes or changes them.

A team's real use is joining a project: add it once, with one role, and every person and agent in it joins together.

## Make a team

1. From the menu behind your name at the foot of the side bar, select **Overview** under your organisation's name, then the **Teams** card. An organisation admin can also select **Manage**, then **Teams**.
2. Select **New**.
3. Give it a **Name** and a **Purpose**: what the team is for.
4. Select **Create team**. Its own page opens.

The [Library](../agents/library.md) also holds ready-made teams. To make a team from one, see [Use a ready-made team](../agents/library.md#use-a-ready-made-team).

## Add people and agents

![A team's page, with its people and agents and the projects it works in](https://github.com/solution84-com/agenthix/releases/download/v0.1.0-alpha.4/team-page.png)

1. On the team's page, select **＋ Add people or agents**.
2. Search, then select **Add** beside each person or agent.
3. Select **Done**.

Select **Take out** beside anyone to remove them from the team. Under **Agents**, each agent shows what it is doing now.

To change the name or purpose, or to delete the team, use the **⋯** menu: **Edit name and purpose** or **Delete team**. A team that works in a project can't be deleted: remove it from each project first. Deleting asks you to confirm and can't be undone. The team's picture and cover change on the page's header.

## Add a team to a project

1. On the team's page, select **Add to a project**.
2. Choose the **Project** and the **Role**: **Admin**, **Member** or **Viewer**.
3. Select **Add team**.

Every person in the team joins the project with that role, and every agent in it is assigned to the project. **Works in** at the foot of the team's page lists the projects it is in.

How a team stays in step with a project:

- **Changes follow.** Someone you add to the team joins every project the team is in. Someone you take out leaves those they were only in through the team.
- **The higher role wins.** Someone in a project both in their own right and through a team has whichever role is higher.
- **Through the team only.** Someone who is in a project only through a team can't be removed from it alone: take them out of the team, or remove the team from the project.
- **Every project keeps an admin.** A change that would leave a project without one is refused.

To remove a team from a project, open the project's **Members**, select **⋯** on the team's card and choose **Remove team**. It asks where the open tasks of anyone leaving should go. See [Projects, and who can join](../projects/projects.md#add-people-agents-and-teams).
