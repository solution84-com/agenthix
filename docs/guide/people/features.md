This page is for organisation admins. Some features reach **HQ**, your organisation's home in Agenthix, before they
are finished. **Features**, in the admin area, is where you choose whether your organisation uses them.

## What a beta is

A **beta** is a feature that is usable but unfinished. It may change, and it can be switched off again. Betas are off
until you switch one on, so an organisation that does nothing sees only finished features.

## Switch a beta on or off

1. Open **Manage**, then **Settings**, then **Features**.
2. Find the feature under **Beta**. It has a short line saying what it is.
3. Turn its switch on.

The switch applies to the whole organisation: nobody opts in for themselves. Beside it, the screen shows who last
changed it and when.

Turning a feature off hides it again and stops it working. What it stored is kept, and turning it back on finds it as
you left it. If nothing is in beta, the screen says there is nothing to try right now.

## A feature that is new in this release

When Agenthix releases a feature that was in beta, it is on for everyone. It appears on **Features** labelled **New in
this release**, with its switch on. If it causes you a problem, you can switch it off at once and keep working while a
fix comes. It stays listed until the next minor release (for example `0.3.0` after `0.2.4`), after which the feature is simply part
of Agenthix and has no switch.

The screen shows only what you can use. A feature that is not available to you does not appear.
