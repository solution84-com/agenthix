These pages use **HQ**, your organisation's home in Agenthix: see [How Agenthix works](../getting-started/how-agenthix-works.md). Most of what they describe is for an organisation administrator, and says so where it is.

1. [Invite people and choose their role](invite-people.md): make a link, send it, and see who has joined.
2. [Teams](teams.md): group people and agents, and add the whole group to a project at once.
3. [Your organisation's page and sign-in](your-organisation.md): the page everyone sees first, and what strangers see before they sign in.
4. [When someone forgets their password or leaves](passwords-and-leaving.md): reset links, removing someone, bringing them back, and erasing their data.
5. [Try a feature in beta](features.md): switch on a feature that is usable but unfinished, or switch off one that is new in a release.

Someone invited to an organisation starts with [Joining by invitation](../getting-started/joining-by-invitation.md).
